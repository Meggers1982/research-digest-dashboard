# Topic Memory: Gut & Digestive Health
_Last updated: 2026-11-02 · source_id: gut-digestive · covers studies through 2026-11-01_

## Established findings
- **sugar-fastfood-long-term-outcomes** — Ultra-processed, sugary and fast-food dietary patterns track with worse long-term outcomes, including depression, earlier cancer onset and incident myeloid neoplasms. (PMIDs 42933535, 42362117, 42508576, 42684964)

## Emerging threads
- **molecular-diagnostics-small-validation** — Diagnostic, prognostic and body-composition measurement tools repeatedly report strong performance in small, single-center, retrospective samples without external validation. (PMIDs 42933650, 42926060, 42863606; first seen 2026-09-04)
- **screening-criteria-determine-detection** — Nutritional, feeding and food-insecurity risk is detected only when standardized or culturally adapted screening tools are applied; otherwise high prevalence stays hidden. (PMIDs 42892325, 42920280, 42921938, 42778586; first seen 2026-09-26)
- **nutrition-supplement-rcts-null** — Randomized additions of protein or probiotics to standard clinical nutrition fail to improve primary outcomes such as muscle thickness or hemoglobin, despite strong observational associations. (PMIDs 42908224, 42933603; first seen 2026-10-26)
- **gut-microbiome-host-response-modifier** — Specific gut microbial features—depleted sulfur-metabolizing taxa, treatment-induced metabolite shifts, single-strain colonization—associate with host disease and treatment response, mostly in observational or secondary analyses. (PMIDs 42919656, 42734327, 42910930; first seen 2026-10-15)
- **gi-mechanism-misattribution** — Gastrointestinal, hepatic and nutritional presentations are often misattributed for years until targeted objective testing, rather than empirical assumption-based management, identifies the true driver. (PMIDs 42875334, 42786208, 42887767, 42905346, 42929334, 41381180; first seen 2026-09-28)
- **ibd-therapy-evidence-gaps** — Biologic selection and dosing in inflammatory bowel disease rests on retrospective, indirect and post hoc analyses, which also show rectal mucosa healing more slowly than proximal colon. (PMIDs 42843624, 42908036, 42905967; first seen 2026-10-14)
- **diet-patterns-chronic-disease-cohorts** — Mediterranean- and DASH-style diet quality scores predict lower cardiovascular and total mortality in cohorts and country-level data, while short behavioral messaging shows no advantage over usual care. (PMIDs 42854785, 42858899, 42697415, 42943027; first seen 2026-09-11)
- **amr-genes-across-reservoirs** — Genomic and surveillance work keeps recovering clinically important antibacterial and antifungal resistance in community, food-chain and animal reservoirs. (PMIDs 42849205, 42822660, 42674221, 42763093, 42875125, 42937308; first seen 2026-09-06)
- **h-pylori-simplified-regimens** — Potassium-competitive acid blocker-based dual therapy matches bismuth quadruple therapy for H. pylori eradication with fewer adverse events and no excess liver injury. (PMIDs 42869649, 42854772, 42802008; first seen 2026-09-28)
- **food-policy-and-environment-interventions** — Fiscal and environmental measures, such as excise taxes on unhealthy imports, shift food supply more than moderation-style messaging, though consumption effects remain unmeasured. (PMIDs 42929274, 42908199; first seen 2026-10-29)
