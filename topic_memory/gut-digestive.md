# Topic Memory: Gut & Digestive Health
_Last updated: 2026-10-05 · source_id: gut-digestive · covers studies through 2026-10-04_

## Established findings
- _None yet._

## Emerging threads
- **molecular-diagnostics-small-validation** — Diagnostic, prognostic and treatment-response markers across gut, nutrition and liver disease are repeatedly reported with strong performance in small, short, exploratory samples lacking external validation. (PMIDs 42800402, 42773017; first seen 2026-09-04)
- **screening-criteria-determine-detection** — Detection of nutritional, feeding and gastrointestinal risk depends on whether standardized screening tools and diagnostic criteria are actually applied, with high hidden prevalence when they are. (PMIDs 42785553, 42778586, 42767497; first seen 2026-09-26)
- **amr-genes-across-reservoirs** — Genomic and culture surveys keep recovering clinically important resistance to first-line therapies in organisms sampled outside hospitals, in community carriage and in food-chain reservoirs. (PMIDs 42822660, 42674221, 40810498, 42763093; first seen 2026-09-06)
- **microbial-enzymatic-food-ingredients** — Microbial, enzymatic and peptide-based food processing improves nutrient profiles and reduces anti-nutritional compounds in laboratory and review work, with no human outcome data yet. (PMIDs 42802379, 42240571; first seen 2026-09-03)
- **masld-noninvasive-metrics-limits** — Baseline BMI, FIB-4 and imaging-based non-invasive tests misclassify fibrosis and prognosis in MASLD and fail as surrogate endpoints, while care shifts toward incretin-based drugs without long-term outcome evidence. (PMIDs 41643801, 42710769, 42684528, 42748406, 42670691; first seen 2026-09-05)
- **sequencing-emerging-pathogens** — Sequencing of clinical and environmental specimens repeatedly flags under-recognized pathogen lineages and undescribed bacterial taxa, while regulatory pathways for metagenomic diagnostics remain unsettled. (PMIDs 42684036, 42695691, 42720301, 42765690, 42800287; first seen 2026-09-02)
- **sugar-fastfood-long-term-outcomes** — High sugar and fast-food dietary patterns track with worse long-term outcomes, including incident depression and anxiety and earlier cancer onset, even as US sugar-sweetened beverage intake has fallen. (PMIDs 42362117, 42508576, 42684964; first seen 2026-09-02)
- **diet-patterns-chronic-disease-cohorts** — Prospective cohorts with repeated dietary data link healthier eating patterns to lower rheumatoid arthritis and hypertensive disorders of pregnancy risk, and delivered MIND-style meals improved cognition in a small trial. (PMIDs 42697415, 42709217, 42772684; first seen 2026-09-11)
- **gi-less-invasive-alternatives** — Feasibility and observational studies suggest less invasive first-step gastrointestinal procedures can spare patients more invasive interventions without evident harm. (PMIDs 42240428, 42471304, 42693590; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **weight-loss-durability-limits** — Weight-loss strategies show limited additive benefit and possible hidden costs, including no synergy from combining time-restricted eating with HIIT and unresolved obesity biology after incretin-driven loss. (PMIDs 42788200, 42754096; first seen 2026-09-24)
- **body-weight-nutrient-metabolites** — Body weight and weight change shape circulating nutrient and metabolite levels, predicting oral vitamin B12 absorption and plasma erythritol concentrations in trial participants. (PMIDs 42768083, 42759771; first seen 2026-09-23)
- **gi-mechanism-misattribution** — Case reports and series describe gastrointestinal and nutritional presentations misattributed to assumed mechanisms, where identifying the true driver changed effective treatment. (PMIDs 42797090, 42786208, 42771130; first seen 2026-09-28)
- **tailored-gi-therapy-and-surveillance** — Resistance-guided tailored H. pylori therapy nearly tripled eradication success over empirical treatment, while post-resection ulcerative colitis neoplasia recurred often, both arguing for test- and surveillance-driven management. (PMIDs 42802008, 42770185; first seen 2026-09-28)
