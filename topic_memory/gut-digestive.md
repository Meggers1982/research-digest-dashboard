# Topic Memory: Gut & Digestive Health
_Last updated: 2026-11-09 · source_id: gut-digestive · covers studies through 2026-11-08_

## Established findings
- **sugar-fastfood-long-term-outcomes** — Ultra-processed, sugary and sweet-heavy dietary patterns track with worse long-term outcomes, including depression, earlier cancer onset, myeloid neoplasms and calcium oxalate kidney stones. (PMIDs 42362117, 42508576, 42684964, 42470774)
- **molecular-diagnostics-small-validation** — Diagnostic, prognostic and body-composition tools repeatedly report strong performance in small, single-center or single-region samples without external validation. (PMIDs 42926060, 42863606, 42963242, 42832973, 42667986)
- **ibd-therapy-evidence-gaps** — Biologic selection, discontinuation and dosing in inflammatory bowel disease rest on retrospective, indirect and post hoc analyses that generally find no difference between strategies. (PMIDs 42843624, 42905967, 42829277, 42944857)

## Emerging threads
- **h-pylori-simplified-regimens** — Acid-blocker plus amoxicillin dual therapy matches bismuth quadruple therapy for H. pylori eradication with fewer adverse events, now supported by pooled randomized trials. (PMIDs 42832287, 42869649; first seen 2026-09-28)
- **amr-genes-across-reservoirs** — Genomic and surveillance work keeps recovering clinically important antibacterial and antifungal resistance in community, food-chain, animal and environmental reservoirs. (PMIDs 42849205, 42822660, 42674221, 42875125, 42962336; first seen 2026-09-06)
- **screening-criteria-determine-detection** — Nutritional, feeding and food-insecurity risk is detected only when standardized or culturally adapted screening tools are applied; otherwise high prevalence stays hidden. (PMIDs 42892325, 42920280, 42921938, 42778586; first seen 2026-09-26)
- **nutrition-supplement-rcts-null** — Randomized additions of protein or probiotics to standard clinical nutrition fail to improve primary outcomes, despite strong observational associations and confused dosing targets. (PMIDs 42908224, 42933603, 42944721; first seen 2026-10-26)
- **gut-microbiome-host-response-modifier** — Gut and intratumoral microbial features are linked to host disease and treatment response, but evidence stays observational, narrative or still unreported. (PMIDs 42910930, 42501555, 42951709; first seen 2026-10-15)
- **gi-mechanism-misattribution** — Gastrointestinal, hepatic and nutritional presentations are often misattributed for years until targeted objective testing identifies the true driver. (PMIDs 42875334, 42786208, 42887767, 42905346, 42929334, 41381180; first seen 2026-09-28)
- **diet-patterns-chronic-disease-cohorts** — Mediterranean- and DASH-style diet quality and higher circulating micronutrients predict lower mortality in cohorts, while short behavioral messaging shows no advantage over usual care. (PMIDs 42854785, 42858899, 42697415, 42943027, 42710783; first seen 2026-09-11)
- **food-policy-and-environment-interventions** — Fiscal and environmental measures, such as excise taxes on unhealthy imports, shift food supply more than moderation-style messaging, though consumption effects remain unmeasured. (PMIDs 42929274, 42908199; first seen 2026-10-29)
- **single-center-surgical-innovation** — Novel gastrointestinal surgical techniques and co-management care models report faster recovery and few complications in uncontrolled single-center series without randomized comparison. (PMIDs 42621732, 42962210, 42711178, 42660346; first seen 2026-11-07)
