# Topic Memory: Gut & Digestive Health
_Last updated: 2026-09-28 · source_id: gut-digestive · covers studies through 2026-09-27_

## Established findings
- _None yet._

## Emerging threads
- **molecular-diagnostics-small-validation** — Diagnostic, prognostic and treatment-response prediction tools across gut and liver disease are repeatedly reported with strong accuracy in small, exploratory or incompletely described samples lacking external validation. (PMIDs 42671187, 42019459, 41213391, 42773017, 42749360; first seen 2026-09-04)
- **masld-therapy-landscape** — Reviews and meeting summaries describe MASLD care shifting toward approved and incretin-based pharmacotherapy, with a crowded pipeline but little long-term outcome evidence. (PMIDs 42670691; first seen 2026-09-04)
- **masld-noninvasive-metrics-limits** — Baseline BMI, FIB-4 and imaging-based non-invasive tests misclassify fibrosis and prognosis in MASLD and fail as validated surrogate endpoints, while tissue transcriptomics points to more consistent fibrosis targets. (PMIDs 41643801, 42710769, 42684528, 42748406; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **amr-genes-across-reservoirs** — Genomic and culture surveys keep recovering clinically important resistance genes, now including novel mobile transposons, in organisms sampled outside hospitals and in food-chain reservoirs. (PMIDs 42593125, 42674221, 40810498, 42763093; first seen 2026-09-06)
- **sequencing-emerging-pathogens** — Sequencing of clinical and environmental specimens repeatedly flags under-recognized pathogen lineages and previously undescribed bacterial taxa, while regulatory pathways for metagenomic diagnostics remain unsettled. (PMIDs 42684036, 42695691, 42720301, 42765690, 42800287; first seen 2026-09-02)
- **sugar-fastfood-long-term-outcomes** — High sugar and fast-food dietary patterns track with worse long-term outcomes, including incident depression and anxiety and earlier cancer onset, even as US sugar-sweetened beverage intake has fallen. (PMIDs 42362117, 42508576, 42684964; first seen 2026-09-02)
- **diet-patterns-chronic-disease-cohorts** — Large prospective cohorts with repeated dietary data link healthier eating patterns to lower rheumatoid arthritis and hypertensive disorders of pregnancy risk, and delivered MIND-style meals improved cognition in a small trial. (PMIDs 42697415, 42709217, 42772684; first seen 2026-09-11)
- **gi-less-invasive-alternatives** — Feasibility and observational studies suggest less invasive first-step gastrointestinal procedures can spare patients more invasive interventions without evident harm. (PMIDs 42240428, 42471304, 42693590; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **microbial-enzymatic-food-ingredients** — Preclinical and review work advances microbial, enzymatic and peptide-based food ingredients and preservatives, with no human outcome data yet. (PMIDs 42569853, 42241827, 42240571, 42772232; first seen 2026-09-03)
- **screening-criteria-determine-detection** — Identification and timely care of patients at nutritional or gastrointestinal risk depend heavily on whether standardized screening tools and diagnostic criteria are actually applied. (PMIDs 42778586, 42765431, 42767497; first seen 2026-09-26)
- **weight-loss-durability-limits** — Weight-loss strategies show limited additive benefit and possible hidden costs: combining time-restricted eating with HIIT gave no synergy and risked lean mass, and incretin-driven loss may not resolve obesity biology. (PMIDs 42788200, 42754096; first seen 2026-09-24)
- **body-weight-nutrient-metabolites** — Body weight and weight change shape circulating nutrient and metabolite levels, predicting oral vitamin B12 absorption and plasma erythritol concentrations in trial participants. (PMIDs 42768083, 42759771; first seen 2026-09-23)
