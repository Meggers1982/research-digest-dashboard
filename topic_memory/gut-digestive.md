## Emerging threads
- **masld-therapy-landscape** — Reviews, commentaries and meeting summaries consistently describe MASLD care moving toward approved and incretin-based pharmacotherapy, with a crowded development pipeline but limited long-term outcome evidence. (PMIDs 42670691; first seen 2026-09-04)
- **amr-genes-across-reservoirs** — Small genomic and culture surveys keep recovering clinically important resistance genes and enteric pathogens outside hospitals — in wildlife, sea turtles and retail meat — with standard detection methods missing some contamination. (PMIDs 42593125, 42674221, 40810498; first seen 2026-09-06)
- **masld-noninvasive-metrics-limits** — Baseline BMI, FIB-4 and imaging-based non-invasive tests misclassify prognosis and fibrosis in MASLD, performing poorly in older adults and failing as validated surrogate endpoints for biopsy in trials. (PMIDs 41643801, 42710769, 42684528; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **sequencing-emerging-pathogens** — Sequencing of clinical specimens repeatedly flags under-recognized pathogens and novel human taxa, including gut species enriched in Parkinson's disease, while regulatory pathways for metagenomic diagnostics remain unsettled. (PMIDs 42684036, 42695691, 42720301; first seen 2026-09-02)
- **sugar-fastfood-long-term-outcomes** — High sugar and fast-food dietary patterns track with worse long-term outcomes, including incident depression and anxiety and earlier cancer onset, even as US sugar-sweetened beverage intake has fallen sharply since 2003. (PMIDs 42362117, 42508576, 42684964; first seen 2026-09-02)
- **diet-patterns-chronic-disease-cohorts** — Large prospective cohorts with repeated food-frequency data link healthier eating patterns — sustained Mediterranean adherence and higher fish intake — to lower rheumatoid arthritis and hypertensive disorders of pregnancy risk. (PMIDs 42697415, 42709217; first seen 2026-09-11)
- **molecular-diagnostics-small-validation** — New diagnostic and risk-prediction tools are reported with strong accuracy in small or incompletely described validation samples, without external validation, across infection testing and reflux symptom triage. (PMIDs 42671187, 42019459, 41213391; first seen 2026-09-04)
- **gi-less-invasive-alternatives** — Feasibility and observational studies suggest less invasive first-step procedures — capsule sponge surveillance, diagnostic ESD before surgery, redesigned paracentesis catheters — can spare patients more invasive interventions without evident harm. (PMIDs 42240428, 42471304, 42693590; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **microbial-enzymatic-food-ingredients** — Preclinical and review work advances microbial and enzymatic production of food ingredients — fermented alternative proteins, thermostable acyltransferases, citrus pomace fiber — with no human outcome data yet. (PMIDs 42569853, 42241827, 42240571; first seen 2026-09-03)
