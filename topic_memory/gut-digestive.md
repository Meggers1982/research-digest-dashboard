# Topic Memory: Gut & Digestive Health

## Emerging threads
- **molecular-diagnostics-small-validation** — Diagnostic, prognostic and exposure markers from stool, plasma and tissue omics repeatedly show strong performance in small, single-center, exploratory samples without external validation. (PMIDs 42863606; first seen 2026-09-04)
- **gi-mechanism-misattribution** — Gastrointestinal and nutritional presentations are repeatedly misattributed to assumed mechanisms, and only objective testing identifies the true driver and changes effective treatment. (PMIDs 42875334, 42786208; first seen 2026-09-28)
- **objective-testing-over-empirical-gi-care** — Measured markers and confirmatory testing—fecal calprotectin in at-risk relatives, resistance-guided H. pylori therapy, motility testing—outperform empirical, assumption-based gastrointestinal management. (PMIDs 41381180, 42802008, 42875334; first seen 2026-09-28)
- **ibd-therapy-evidence-gaps** — First-line biologic selection in Crohn's disease and pediatric ulcerative colitis rests on retrospective and indirect data, with vedolizumab matching anti-TNF effectiveness and fewer adverse events. (PMIDs 42843624, 42850668; first seen 2026-10-14)
- **masld-noninvasive-metrics-limits** — Non-invasive MASLD metrics misclassify fibrosis and prognosis and remain unvalidated as surrogates, though early-pregnancy elastography-detected MASLD tracks with later hypertensive disorders. (PMIDs 42863812, 41643801; first seen 2026-09-05)
- **diet-patterns-chronic-disease-cohorts** — Prospective cohorts tie Mediterranean-style lifestyle and healthier diets to lower mortality and chronic disease risk, while short behavioral messaging trials show no advantage over usual care. (PMIDs 42854785, 42858899, 42697415; first seen 2026-09-11)
- **amr-genes-across-reservoirs** — Genomic and culture surveys keep recovering clinically important resistance to first-line therapies in community carriage and food-chain reservoirs outside hospitals. (PMIDs 42849205, 42822660, 42674221, 40810498, 42763093; first seen 2026-09-06)
- **screening-criteria-determine-detection** — Detection of nutritional, feeding and gastrointestinal risk depends on whether standardized screening tools and culturally adapted criteria are applied, with high hidden prevalence when they are. (PMIDs 42833303, 42785553, 42778586, 42767497; first seen 2026-09-26)
- **h-pylori-simplified-regimens** — Simplified potassium-competitive acid blocker-based dual therapy matches bismuth quadruple therapy for H. pylori eradication with fewer adverse events, while P-CABs show no excess liver injury. (PMIDs 42869649, 42854772, 42802008; first seen 2026-09-28)
- **gut-microbiome-host-response-modifier** — Gut microbiome composition and diet are proposed as modifiers of host treatment response and disease risk, so far supported mainly by reviews and observational associations. (PMIDs 42849841, 42862416, 42695691; first seen 2026-10-15)
- **sugar-fastfood-long-term-outcomes** — High sugar and fast-food dietary patterns track with worse long-term outcomes, including incident depression and anxiety and earlier cancer onset. (PMIDs 42362117, 42508576, 42684964; first seen 2026-09-02)
- **gi-less-invasive-alternatives** — Feasibility and observational studies suggest less invasive first-step gastrointestinal procedures can spare patients more invasive interventions without evident harm. (PMIDs 42240428, 42471304, 42693590; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **body-weight-nutrient-metabolites** — Body weight, weight change and inflammatory lipoproteins shape circulating nutrient and metabolite levels and response to supplementation. (PMIDs 42833517, 42768083, 42759771; first seen 2026-09-23)
- **weight-loss-durability-limits** — Weight-loss strategies show limited additive benefit and hidden costs, with postpartum weight retention instead tracking breastfeeding duration in cohort trajectory data. (PMIDs 42863706, 42788200, 42754096; first seen 2026-09-24)
