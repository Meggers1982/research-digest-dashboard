# Topic Memory: Science & Environment
_Last updated: 2026-09-28 · source_id: science-environment · covers studies through 2026-09-27_

## Established findings
- **amr-novel-determinants** — Genomic surveillance of small clinical isolate sets keeps uncovering new or newly mobile resistance determinants, from dual-resistant gonococci and KPC/NDM Klebsiella to a novel NDM-1 transposon and resistant HIV recombinants. (PMIDs 42615933, 42628851, 42660317, 42763093, 42786982)
- **candida-auris-spread** — Candidozyma (Candida) auris keeps causing hard-to-contain hospital outbreaks, while disinfectant efficacy testing based on C. albicans may overstate killing. (PMIDs 42600976, 42655933, 42570726)
- **arbovirus-vector-expansion** — Surveillance keeps documenting vector-borne and zoonotic pathogens in new territory, hosts or settings, from urban Chagas vectors to tick-borne flaviviruses and first national detections of zoonotic parasites. (PMIDs 42611910, 42660212, 42690390, 42486427, 42746917)
- **microplastics-ubiquitous-unregulated** — Routine measurement underestimates chemical exposure: microplastics reach groundwater and tissue, monitoring misses storm-driven pesticide pulses, biomarker mixture analysis exceeds neurotoxic thresholds, and dietary surveys undercount selenium. (PMIDs 42611205, 42772167, 42785006)
- **antibiotic-prescribing-mismatch** — Real-world antibiotic prescribing often mismatches guidelines and susceptibility data across settings, from cystitis to pediatric urgent care and bacteremia. (PMIDs 42565893, 42679212, 42575218, 42594063)
- **design-flaws-inflate-efficacy** — Meta-research finds treatment and policy conclusions inflated or fragile from biased trial designs, one dominant primary study, disputed historical data, and industry-convened consensus panels. (PMIDs 42600710, 42600711, 42658107, 42430709)
- **broad-sequencing-beyond-panels** — Exome, genome and RNA-level analyses outperform targeted panels, resolving rare disease diagnoses that panels missed or misassigned. (PMIDs 42615209, 42667410, 42624176, 42675022, 42687628)
- **ancestry-gaps-variant-databases** — Sequencing families from ancestries underrepresented in genetic databases repeatedly yields novel or reclassified pathogenic variants for rare blindness, deafness and infertility. (PMIDs 42706666, 42339879, 42758422, 42761588, 42768778, 42760497, 42768956)

## Emerging threads
- **pesticide-resistance-erosion** — Chemical control of pests and vectors is losing ground to newly detected resistance mutations in nematodes, crop pests and mosquitoes, pushing biological and genetic alternatives. (PMIDs 42376859, 42764124; first seen 2026-09-03)
- **structural-determinants-outcomes** — Area-level, ethnic and migration-related exposures track with worse health outcomes and access, from breast cancer survival to screening kit rejection and immigrant autistic well-being. (PMIDs 42237574, 41995389, 42587204, 42704432, 42752825; first seen 2026-08-10)
- **model-assumptions-inflate-burden** — Conventional attributable-fraction and epidemic models overestimate infection burden and epidemic size when behavioral change and individual heterogeneity are ignored. (PMIDs 42149607, 42691106, 42700587; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **pathogens-unexpected-adult-populations** — Pathogens long considered pediatric are appearing in adults, with invasive Hib in unhoused adults who use drugs and off-season enterovirus D68 in older adults. (PMIDs 42695188, 42584091; first seen 2026-09-08)
