# Topic Memory: Science & Environment
_Last updated: 2026-10-26 · source_id: science-environment · covers studies through 2026-10-25_

## Established findings
- **amr-novel-determinants** — Genomic surveillance of small clinical isolate sets keeps uncovering new or newly mobile resistance determinants across bacteria, fungi and viruses. (PMIDs 42615933, 42628851, 42660317, 42763093, 42786982, 42822660, 41834339, 42433029)
- **arbovirus-vector-expansion** — Surveillance keeps documenting vector-borne, rodent-borne and zoonotic pathogens in new vectors, territories, hosts and patient groups. (PMIDs 42611910, 42690390, 42746917, 42825336, 42660107, 42858726, 42875844)
- **microplastics-ubiquitous-unregulated** — Routine monitoring, certification and self-report understate real chemical exposure, while targeted sampling and biomarkers find contamination where regulation assumes little. (PMIDs 42611205, 42772167, 42785006, 42805074, 42892516, 42899791, 42912073)
- **antibiotic-prescribing-mismatch** — Antibiotic choice is routinely made empirically before diagnostic or susceptibility results, with large unexplained variation in overuse across hospitals and settings. (PMIDs 42565893, 42679212, 42575218, 42594063, 42345285)
- **design-flaws-inflate-efficacy** — Meta-research finds treatment, cost-effectiveness and synthesis conclusions inflated or fragile from biased designs, drifting review methods and flawed umbrella reviews. (PMIDs 42600711, 42658107, 42430709, 42838152, 42853372, 42896684)
- **broad-sequencing-beyond-panels** — Exome, genome, RNA-level and functional analyses outperform targeted panels, resolving novel disease genes, unexpected phenotype reversals, dual diagnoses and panel-negative rare disease. (PMIDs 42615209, 42667410, 42624176, 42687628, 42831386, 42891780, 42891915)
- **ancestry-gaps-variant-databases** — Sequencing families from ancestries underrepresented in genetic databases, often consanguineous, repeatedly yields founder, novel or reclassified pathogenic variants. (PMIDs 42706666, 42339879, 42758422, 42761588, 42768778, 42873299, 42847748)
- **structural-determinants-outcomes** — Deprivation, ethnicity, migration and minority status predict infection risk and access to diagnosis, treatment and prevention even within universal health systems. (PMIDs 42237574, 42587204, 42704432, 42776600, 42897406, 42907707)
- **molecular-methods-beat-culture** — Conventional culture and phenotypic tests miss or misassign pathogens, including when resistant flora overgrow them, that sequencing, multiplex PCR or modified media detect. (PMIDs 42803991, 42788738, 42808754, 42822183, 42864586, 42892438)

## Emerging threads
- **pesticide-resistance-erosion** — Field control of vectors and crop pests is eroding, through newly detected resistance mutations and a collapsed Wolbachia release that left infection far below suppression levels. (PMIDs 42376859, 42764124, 42848677; first seen 2026-09-03)
- **model-assumptions-inflate-burden** — Conventional attributable-fraction and epidemic models overestimate infection burden and epidemic size when behavioral change and individual heterogeneity are ignored. (PMIDs 42149607, 42691106, 42700587; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **postmarketing-safety-detection-lag** — Drug safety warnings emerge only after very large patient exposure, and post-marketing signals are distorted by confounding, reporting-rule confusion and analytic pitfalls. (PMIDs 42565496, 42283795, 42805881, 42803717; first seen 2026-10-04)
- **implementation-gap-primary-care** — In low- and middle-income primary care, stocked commodities and legally available services often go undelivered because frontline staff refer away or avoid providing them. (PMIDs 42852811, 42660105, 42853554; first seen 2026-10-14)
- **informal-occupational-exposure-unprotected** — Informal and institutional workers carry heavy exposure-related symptom burdens, with risk scaling by cumulative hours and differing by sex and product batch. (PMIDs 42908158, 42899791; first seen 2026-10-24)
