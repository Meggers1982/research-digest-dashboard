# Topic Memory: Science & Environment
_Last updated: 2026-11-02 · source_id: science-environment · covers studies through 2026-11-01_

## Established findings
- **amr-novel-determinants** — Genomic surveillance of small clinical isolate sets keeps uncovering new or newly mobile resistance determinants and imported resistant clones across bacteria, fungi and viruses. (PMIDs 42615933, 42628851, 42660317, 42763093, 42786982, 42822660, 42762688)
- **arbovirus-vector-expansion** — Surveillance keeps documenting vector-borne, rodent-borne and zoonotic pathogens in new vectors, territories, hosts and patient groups. (PMIDs 42611910, 42690390, 42746917, 42825336, 42912015, 42914702, 42904687, 42927799)
- **microplastics-ubiquitous-unregulated** — Routine monitoring, certification and self-report understate real chemical exposure, while targeted sampling and biomarkers find contamination where regulation assumes little. (PMIDs 42611205, 42772167, 42785006, 42805074, 42892516, 42899791, 42913773)
- **antibiotic-prescribing-mismatch** — Antibiotic choice is routinely made empirically before diagnostic or susceptibility results, with large unexplained variation in overuse across hospitals and settings. (PMIDs 42565893, 42679212, 42575218, 42594063, 42345285)
- **design-flaws-inflate-efficacy** — Meta-research finds treatment, cost-effectiveness and synthesis conclusions inflated or fragile from biased designs, arbitrary analytic choices, undefined constructs and thin primary literature. (PMIDs 42600711, 42658107, 42430709, 42838152, 42909809, 42928544, 42963525)
- **broad-sequencing-beyond-panels** — Exome, genome, RNA-level and functional analyses outperform targeted panels, resolving novel disease genes, unexpected phenotype reversals, dual diagnoses and panel-negative rare disease. (PMIDs 42615209, 42667410, 42624176, 42687628, 42831386, 42918961, 42895690, 42910509)
- **ancestry-gaps-variant-databases** — Sequencing families and cohorts from ancestries underrepresented in genetic databases, often consanguineous, repeatedly yields founder, novel or reclassified variants, while polygenic scores transfer poorly. (PMIDs 42706666, 42339879, 42758422, 42761588, 42926074, 42960730, 42503805)
- **structural-determinants-outcomes** — Deprivation, ethnicity, migration, urban environment and diagnostic coverage predict health outcomes and access, and can create apparent paradoxes that are measurement artifacts. (PMIDs 42237574, 42587204, 42704432, 42950439, 42926302, 42905202, 42901478)
- **molecular-methods-beat-culture** — Conventional culture, panels and clinical criteria miss or misassign pathogens that sequencing, droplet culture, multiplex assays or new antigen tests detect. (PMIDs 42803991, 42788738, 42808754, 42895324, 42905455)

## Emerging threads
- **pesticide-resistance-erosion** — Field control of vectors and crop pests is eroding, through newly detected resistance mutations and a collapsed Wolbachia release that left infection far below suppression levels. (PMIDs 42376859, 42764124, 42848677; first seen 2026-09-03)
- **model-assumptions-inflate-burden** — Conventional attributable-fraction and epidemic models overestimate infection burden and epidemic size when behavioral change and individual heterogeneity are ignored. (PMIDs 42149607, 42691106, 42700587; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **postmarketing-safety-detection-lag** — Drug risks and benefits stay unresolved long after approval, with thin registry data, confounded signals and prescribing shifts driven by guidance rather than evidence. (PMIDs 42565496, 42283795, 42908038, 42918487; first seen 2026-10-04)
- **implementation-gap-primary-care** — In low- and middle-income primary care, stocked commodities and legally available services often go undelivered because frontline staff refer away or avoid providing them. (PMIDs 42852811, 42660105, 42853554, 42906415; first seen 2026-10-14)
- **informal-occupational-exposure-unprotected** — Workers in informal, clinical-training and agricultural settings carry heavy exposure and ergonomic risk while safety documentation and support systems remain incomplete. (PMIDs 42908158, 42899791, 42904767, 42949366, 42908437; first seen 2026-10-24)
