# Topic Memory: Science & Environment
_Last updated: 2026-10-05 · source_id: science-environment · covers studies through 2026-10-04_

## Established findings
- **amr-novel-determinants** — Genomic surveillance of small clinical isolate sets keeps uncovering new or newly mobile resistance determinants, from dual-resistant gonococci and KPC/NDM Klebsiella to mobile nimK in vaginal Prevotella, CMV UL97/UL54 mutations and resistant HIV recombinants. (PMIDs 42615933, 42628851, 42660317, 42763093, 42786982, 42822660, 41834339, 42433029)
- **candida-auris-spread** — Candidozyma (Candida) auris keeps causing hard-to-contain hospital outbreaks, while disinfectant efficacy testing based on C. albicans may overstate killing of some clades. (PMIDs 42600976, 42655933, 42570726)
- **arbovirus-vector-expansion** — Surveillance keeps documenting vector-borne and zoonotic pathogens in new territory, hosts or settings, including Oropouche beyond Amazonia, dengue in the high Himalayas, Aedes aegypti in a new Saudi city and a second Shamonda clade in European cattle. (PMIDs 42611910, 42660212, 42690390, 42486427, 42746917, 42296443, 42324765, 42461315, 42825336)
- **microplastics-ubiquitous-unregulated** — Routine measurement underestimates chemical exposure: microplastics reach groundwater and tissue, storm pesticide pulses are missed, and targeted assays find replacement PFAS, flame retardants in eggs and metals in edible fish. (PMIDs 42611205, 42772167, 42785006, 42805074, 42790853, 42773322)
- **antibiotic-prescribing-mismatch** — Real-world antibiotic prescribing often mismatches guidelines and susceptibility data across settings, from cystitis to pediatric urgent care and bacteremia. (PMIDs 42565893, 42679212, 42575218, 42594063)
- **design-flaws-inflate-efficacy** — Meta-research finds treatment and policy conclusions inflated or fragile from biased trial designs, one dominant primary study, disputed historical data, and industry-convened consensus panels. (PMIDs 42600710, 42600711, 42658107, 42430709)
- **broad-sequencing-beyond-panels** — Exome, genome and RNA-level analyses outperform targeted panels, resolving rare disease diagnoses that panels missed or misassigned. (PMIDs 42615209, 42667410, 42624176, 42675022, 42687628)
- **ancestry-gaps-variant-databases** — Sequencing families from ancestries underrepresented in genetic databases repeatedly yields novel or reclassified pathogenic variants for rare blindness, deafness and infertility. (PMIDs 42706666, 42339879, 42758422, 42761588, 42768778, 42760497, 42768956)

## Emerging threads
- **structural-determinants-outcomes** — Area-level, ethnic, migration and displacement-related exposures track with worse health outcomes and access, from breast cancer survival and screening uptake to refugee vaccination coverage and conflict-zone disease burden. (PMIDs 42237574, 41995389, 42587204, 42704432, 42752825, 42776600, 42785455; first seen 2026-08-10)
- **pesticide-resistance-erosion** — Chemical control of pests and vectors is losing ground to newly detected resistance mutations in nematodes, crop pests and mosquitoes, pushing biological and genetic alternatives. (PMIDs 42376859, 42764124; first seen 2026-09-03)
- **model-assumptions-inflate-burden** — Conventional attributable-fraction and epidemic models overestimate infection burden and epidemic size when behavioral change and individual heterogeneity are ignored. (PMIDs 42149607, 42691106, 42700587; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **molecular-methods-beat-culture** — Conventional culture and phenotypic identification miss or misassign microbes that multiplex PCR, direct clinical sequencing and droplet microfluidic cultivation detect, in clinical and environmental samples alike. (PMIDs 42820865, 42803991, 42788738, 42808754, 42822183; first seen 2026-10-02)
- **postmarketing-safety-detection-lag** — Drug safety warnings emerge only after very large patient exposure, and post-marketing signals are distorted by confounding, reporting-rule confusion and analytic pitfalls in spontaneous adverse-event data. (PMIDs 42565496, 42283795, 42805881, 42803717; first seen 2026-10-04)
