# Topic Memory: Science & Environment
_Last updated: 2026-11-09 · source_id: science-environment · covers studies through 2026-11-08_

## Established findings
- **amr-novel-determinants** — Genomic and wastewater surveillance of small clinical sample sets keeps uncovering newly emerged resistant clones, recombinant viruses, imported lineages and unnamed species ahead of routine diagnostics. (PMIDs 42615933, 42660317, 42763093, 42822660, 42762688, 42986214, 42909847, 42993182, 42968272)
- **arbovirus-vector-expansion** — Surveillance keeps documenting vector-borne, rodent-borne and zoonotic pathogens, plus resistance-gene reservoirs, in new vectors, hosts, territories and companion, farm and wild animal populations. (PMIDs 42690390, 42825336, 42912015, 42927799, 42934956, 42962336, 42986108)
- **microplastics-ubiquitous-unregulated** — Routine monitoring and certification understate real chemical exposure, while targeted sampling and biomarkers find unregulated contaminants in water, tissue and remote environments. (PMIDs 42611205, 42785006, 42892516, 42913773, 42934473, 42702419, 42962469)
- **antibiotic-prescribing-mismatch** — Antibiotic choice is routinely empirical, with published regional resistance data poorly predicting real-world cure and large unexplained variation in use across hospitals and patient groups. (PMIDs 42565893, 42679212, 42575218, 42984476, 42952123)
- **design-flaws-inflate-efficacy** — Biased designs, arbitrary analytic choices and conventional models inflate or distort treatment effects and disease burden, and newly proposed frameworks shift the conclusions. (PMIDs 42600711, 42838152, 42928544, 42963525, 42149607, 42700587, 42484317, 42785440)
- **broad-sequencing-beyond-panels** — Exome, genome and functional analyses outperform targeted panels, resolving panel-negative rare disease, novel disease genes, dual diagnoses and unexpected phenotype expansions. (PMIDs 42615209, 42687628, 42918961, 42910509, 42960537, 42949432, 42945972, 42307204)
- **ancestry-gaps-variant-databases** — Sequencing consanguineous and non-European families repeatedly yields novel, founder or reclassified variants and unexpected gene–phenotype links, while polygenic scores transfer poorly across ancestries. (PMIDs 42706666, 42926074, 42960730, 42503805, 42768778, 42965905, 42758422)
- **structural-determinants-outcomes** — Deprivation, ethnicity, rurality, migration and digital design predict health outcomes and access, and can create apparent paradoxes that are measurement artifacts. (PMIDs 42237574, 42704432, 42950439, 42905202, 42944827, 42762708, 42762121, 42901191)
- **molecular-methods-beat-culture** — Culture, clinical criteria and standard laboratory tests miss or misassign disease that sequencing, autopsy, biomarker panels and multiplex assays detect. (PMIDs 42803991, 42788738, 42895324, 42905455, 42945424, 42946591, 42852489)
- **pesticide-resistance-erosion** — Field control of vectors and crop pests is eroding through insecticide resistance mutations, mosquito behavioral adaptation and failed biological releases, with weak regional monitoring capacity. (PMIDs 42764124, 42848677, 42961946, 42551822)

## Emerging threads
- **fungal-threat-expansion** — Multidrug-resistant and environmental fungi are diversifying genotypically and causing invasive disease in hosts and care settings once considered low-risk, with azole treatment increasingly failing. (PMIDs 42825548, 42655933, 42875125, 42908349, 42911143; first seen 2026-11-03)
- **ai-model-performance-overstated** — Machine learning tools in chemistry, toxicology and peptide design report accuracy inflated by data leakage or lacking experimental validation, so they are not yet safety-grade. (PMIDs 42962734, 42752649, 42594696; first seen 2026-11-06)
- **implementation-gap-primary-care** — Available services and protective procedures go undelivered at the frontline for workflow, behavioral and organizational reasons rather than missing supplies or equipment. (PMIDs 42852811, 42660105, 42853554, 42906415, 42956938, 42521087; first seen 2026-10-14)
- **postmarketing-safety-detection-lag** — Drug risks, benefits and subgroup effects stay unresolved long after approval, surfacing only in long-term observational follow-up or reanalysis of old trials. (PMIDs 42565496, 42908038, 42918487, 42991121, 42399413, 42714746; first seen 2026-10-04)
