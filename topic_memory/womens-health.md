# Topic Memory: Women's Health
_Last updated: 2026-09-14 · source_id: womens-health · covers studies through 2026-09-13_

## Established findings
- **lactation-support-infrastructure-gaps** — Studies from the US, India, Ethiopia and humanitarian settings document thin lactation-support infrastructure: few breastfeeding-medicine divisions, little technique counseling despite prenatal contact, frontline skill gaps, and midwives excluded from emergency planning. (PMIDs 42596659, 42613089, 42563452, 42624069)
- **milk-composition-reflects-maternal-intake-exposure** — Maternal diet, medication and chemical exposures are measurably reflected in human milk, with fatty acids tracking recent intake, parabens and everolimus detected, and specific glycans linked to infant bronchitis and allergy. (PMIDs 42640325, 42641861, 42634380, 42566066)
- **female-sex-psychiatric-burden** — Female sex marks heavier psychiatric burden: eating-disorder diagnoses rose in girls after school closures, female sex topped suicide-attempt prediction, childhood loneliness raised psychosis risk more in girls, and war PTSD burden was greater in women. (PMIDs 42576300, 42562219, 42636467, 42617370)
- **first-trimester-blood-markers-obstetric-risk** — First-trimester screening using blood-count and hormonal markers, blood pressure or uterine artery Doppler predicts gestational diabetes, large-for-gestational-age birth and preeclampsia, including twin pregnancies, but accuracy is modest and external validation is missing. (PMIDs 42400128, 42723529, 42082866, 42492475)
- **food-labels-mislead-consumers** — Consumers misjudge packaged food and drink: beverage sugar is widely underestimated, supermarket products labeled 'homemade' carry many additives, and nutrition claims on alcohol sway drinkers, women more than men. (PMIDs 42586419, 42658490, 42581643)
- **metabolic-risk-before-antipsychotics** — In drug-naive first-episode psychosis and mania, elevated fasting insulin, altered inflammatory indices and abnormal resting brain activity appear before any antipsychotic exposure. (PMIDs 42691514, 42664610, 42112712, 42692293, 42579964)

## Emerging threads
- **diet-biomarkers-beat-self-report** — Blood metabolomic and proteomic diet markers correct questionnaire error and predict long-term disease, linking dietary patterns to cardiovascular disease, gout and breast and colorectal cancer in adults including postmenopausal women. (PMIDs 42705543, 42679899; first seen 2026-09-03)
- **autism-family-caregiver-strain** — Caregivers of autistic children, mostly mothers, report heavy strain: stress driven by parental attitudes rather than symptom severity, mealtime burden, unmet support in transitions, and systemic barriers for First Nations families. (PMIDs 42517456, 42609138; first seen 2026-08-19)
- **donor-milk-access-inequity** — US reports suggest donor human milk is distributed unevenly, favoring White, English-speaking, privately insured families in newborn nurseries, and is hard to obtain or get covered for medically complex infants at home. (PMIDs 42554280, 42613792; first seen 2026-08-12)
- **endometrial-cancer-adc-targets** — Tissue microarray studies of endometrial carcinoma point to TROP2 as a near-universal antibody-drug-conjugate target, while folate receptor alpha meets the ovarian eligibility threshold in only about 3% of tumors. (PMIDs 42567118, 42565327; first seen 2026-08-11)
- **sex-hormonal-status-psychosis-treatment** — In first-episode psychosis, sex and hormonal status may shape treatment: postmenopausal women needed higher olanzapine doses, and prolactin–glucose relationships ran in opposite directions in men and women. (PMIDs 42636473, 42696132; first seen 2026-08-27)
- **meal-order-postprandial-glucose** — Two small randomized crossover trials found eating carbohydrates last lowered postprandial glucose peaks, in Korean women at metabolic risk and in male athletes, though athletes reported more hunger. (PMIDs 42608327, 42617805; first seen 2026-08-23)
- **postpartum-pelvic-floor-morbidity** — Vaginal birth leaves under-recognized pelvic floor morbidity: about one in eight women still reported perineal pain at three months, and levator ani gene expression changed differently in older primiparas. (PMIDs 42686059, 41997518; first seen 2026-09-08)
