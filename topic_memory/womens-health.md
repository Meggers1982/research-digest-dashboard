# Topic Memory: Women's Health
_Last updated: 2026-10-05 · source_id: womens-health · covers studies through 2026-10-04_

## Established findings
- **lactation-support-infrastructure-gaps** — Perinatal clinicians across the US, India, Ethiopia, humanitarian settings and central Europe report missing training, counseling time and service structures for breastfeeding support, including for mothers with obesity. (PMIDs 42596659, 42613089, 42563452, 42624069, 42296620)
- **milk-composition-reflects-maternal-intake-exposure** — Maternal diet, medication and chemical exposures are measurably reflected in human milk, with fatty acids tracking recent intake, parabens and everolimus detected, and specific glycans linked to infant bronchitis and allergy. (PMIDs 42640325, 42641861, 42634380, 42566066)
- **female-sex-psychiatric-burden** — Female sex marks earlier and heavier psychiatric burden, with self-harm peaking in adolescent girls, rising eating-disorder diagnoses, and steep post-diagnosis self-harm risk in women with ADHD, PTSD or eating disorders. (PMIDs 42785388, 42617370, 42134004)
- **first-trimester-blood-markers-obstetric-risk** — First-trimester blood-count, hormonal, blood pressure and Doppler markers predict gestational diabetes, large-for-gestational-age birth and preeclampsia, but accuracy is modest and external validation is missing. (PMIDs 42400128, 42723529, 42082866, 42492475)
- **metabolic-risk-before-antipsychotics** — In first-episode psychosis with little or no antipsychotic exposure, elevated fasting insulin, altered inflammatory and prolactin profiles and abnormal brain imaging markers are already present. (PMIDs 42691514, 42112712, 42800290)
- **perinatal-maternal-depression-child-outcomes** — Maternal perinatal mental illness, from depressive symptoms to psychiatric hospitalization, predicts worse child outcomes including developmental delay, less favorable temperament and more potentially avoidable hospitalizations. (PMIDs 42778033, 42779328, 42811250)
- **postpartum-pelvic-floor-morbidity** — Perineal and pelvic floor morbidity after vaginal birth persists for months, and positional pushing techniques or platelet-rich plasma lack evidence of benefit. (PMIDs 42686059, 41997518, 42771819, 42807524)

## Emerging threads
- **diet-biomarkers-beat-self-report** — Blood metabolomic and proteomic markers of diet correct questionnaire error, revealing underestimated nutrient intake and predicting cardiometabolic and cancer outcomes in adults including postmenopausal women. (PMIDs 42785006, 42679899; first seen 2026-09-03)
- **donor-milk-access-inequity** — US reports suggest donor human milk is distributed unevenly, favoring White, English-speaking, privately insured families, and is hard to obtain or get covered for medically complex infants at home. (PMIDs 42554280, 42613792; first seen 2026-08-12)
- **endometrial-cancer-adc-targets** — Tissue microarray studies of endometrial carcinoma point to TROP2 as a near-universal antibody-drug-conjugate target, while folate receptor alpha meets the ovarian eligibility threshold in only about 3% of tumors. (PMIDs 42567118, 42565327; first seen 2026-08-11)
- **sex-hormonal-status-psychosis-treatment** — In first-episode psychosis, sex and hormonal status may shape treatment and biology: postmenopausal women needed higher olanzapine doses, and prolactin relationships with glucose and cytokines differ by group. (PMIDs 42636473, 42696132, 42800290; first seen 2026-08-27)
- **endometriosis-detection-central-pain** — Transvaginal ultrasound detects ovarian endometriomas well but misses deep infiltrating disease, which is also marked by altered resting-state brain connectivity in women with chronic pelvic pain. (PMIDs 42808571, 42033133; first seen 2026-10-04)
