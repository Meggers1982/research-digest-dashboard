# Topic Memory: Women's Health
_Last updated: 2026-11-02 · source_id: womens-health · covers studies through 2026-11-01_

## Established findings
- **lactation-support-infrastructure-gaps** — Breastfeeding continuation fails where external supports are missing — clinician training and counseling time, hospital access, tolerable heat, and specialist guidance for medically complex infants — and digital milk-volume monitors are no substitute. (PMIDs 42596659, 42613089, 42563452, 42844698, 42867598, 42924617)
- **milk-composition-reflects-maternal-intake-exposure** — Maternal diet, medication, chemical and substance exposures are measurably reflected in human milk, including fatty acids, parabens, everolimus, glycans linked to infant illness, and immune factors altered by combined cannabis-nicotine use. (PMIDs 42641861, 42634380, 42899680)
- **female-sex-psychiatric-burden** — Female sex marks earlier and heavier psychiatric burden, with self-harm peaking in adolescent girls, rising eating-disorder diagnoses, and steep post-diagnosis self-harm risk in women with ADHD, PTSD or eating disorders. (PMIDs 42785388, 42617370, 42134004)
- **metabolic-risk-before-antipsychotics** — In first-episode psychosis with little or no antipsychotic exposure, elevated fasting insulin, altered inflammatory, metabolomic and prolactin profiles and abnormal brain imaging markers are already present. (PMIDs 42691514, 42112712, 42800290, 42925955)
- **sex-hormonal-status-psychosis-treatment** — In first-episode psychosis, sex and hormonal status shape biology and treatment: sex-specific genetic effects on verbal memory disappear with illness, and postmenopausal women needed higher olanzapine doses. (PMIDs 42636473, 42696132, 42800290, 42843653)
- **perinatal-maternal-depression-child-outcomes** — Maternal perinatal mental illness predicts worse child outcomes including developmental delay, less favorable temperament, altered mother-infant interaction and more potentially avoidable hospitalizations. (PMIDs 42778033, 42779328, 42811250, 42891960)
- **postpartum-pelvic-floor-morbidity** — Perineal and pelvic floor morbidity after vaginal birth persists for months, and positional pushing techniques or platelet-rich plasma lack evidence of benefit. (PMIDs 42686059, 41997518, 42771819, 42807524)
- **incretin-trials-lack-sex-disaggregated-data** — Incretin drug trials and reviews report pooled efficacy and patient-reported effects without sex-disaggregated outcomes, even though female sex is the leading risk factor for GLP-1-associated hair loss. (PMIDs 42843337, 42875600, 42850058, 42873315, 42897102)
- **first-trimester-blood-markers-obstetric-risk** — First-trimester blood markers predict gestational diabetes, large-for-gestational-age birth and preeclampsia only modestly, and European-derived preeclampsia algorithms require recalibration in Chinese pregnancies. (PMIDs 42723529, 42839342, 42919680, 42452901)

## Emerging threads
- **endometriosis-detection-central-pain** — Transvaginal ultrasound detects ovarian endometriomas well but misses deep lesions; adding MRI raises sensitivity for rectosigmoid and uterosacral disease while lowering specificity, and central pain processing is altered in affected women. (PMIDs 42808571, 42922369, 42033133; first seen 2026-10-04)
- **ivf-predictors-fail-prospective-testing** — Assisted-reproduction prognostic and genetic tests underperform on prospective retesting: endometrial thickness fails to predict implantation and nearly half of embryos called 'chaotic' by niPGT-A reclassify as normal. (PMIDs 42926142, 42937235; first seen 2026-10-25)
- **monogenic-variants-male-infertility** — Whole-exome sequencing of men with azoospermia or unexplained infertility keeps identifying novel single-gene variants, including ADGRG2 and ZPBP, that explain previously idiopathic cases. (PMIDs 42895690, 42920325; first seen 2026-10-26)
- **bmi-misses-nutritional-risk** — BMI fails to identify malnutrition and muscle loss in hospitalized adults and chronically ill children, which GLIM criteria, DXA/bioimpedance and mid-upper arm circumference detect and link to mortality. (PMIDs 42956894, 42926060, 42475886; first seen 2026-10-28)
