# Topic Memory: Women's Health
_Last updated: 2026-11-09 · source_id: womens-health · covers studies through 2026-11-08_

## Established findings
- **lactation-support-infrastructure-gaps** — Breastfeeding continuation fails where external supports are missing — clinician training and counseling time, hospital access, tolerable heat, and specialist guidance for medically complex infants — and digital milk-volume monitors are no substitute. (PMIDs 42596659, 42613089, 42563452, 42844698, 42867598, 42924617)
- **milk-composition-reflects-maternal-intake-exposure** — Maternal diet, medication, chemical and substance exposures are measurably reflected in human milk, including fatty acids, parabens, everolimus, glycans linked to infant illness, and immune factors altered by combined cannabis-nicotine use. (PMIDs 42641861, 42634380, 42899680)
- **female-sex-psychiatric-burden** — Female sex marks earlier and heavier psychiatric burden, with self-harm peaking in adolescent girls, rising eating-disorder diagnoses, steep post-diagnosis self-harm risk, and greater inhibitory-control difficulty driving anxiety in autistic women. (PMIDs 42785388, 42617370, 42134004, 40381091)
- **metabolic-risk-before-antipsychotics** — In first-episode psychosis with little or no antipsychotic exposure, inflammatory indices, prolactin, metabolomic profiles and diffusion imaging markers are already abnormal, and baseline immune profile predicts later antipsychotic-associated weight gain. (PMIDs 42691514, 42112712, 42925955, 42305038, 42800290, 42664610)
- **sex-hormonal-status-psychosis-treatment** — In first-episode psychosis, sex and hormonal status shape biology and treatment: sex-specific genetic effects on verbal memory disappear with illness, and postmenopausal women needed higher olanzapine doses. (PMIDs 42636473, 42696132, 42800290, 42843653)
- **perinatal-maternal-depression-child-outcomes** — Maternal perinatal mental illness predicts worse child outcomes including developmental delay, less favorable temperament and avoidable hospitalizations, though a Norwegian sibling-design study attributes the school-achievement association largely to shared family factors. (PMIDs 42778033, 42779328, 42811250, 42891960, 42340381)
- **postpartum-pelvic-floor-morbidity** — Perineal and pelvic floor morbidity after vaginal birth persists for months, affecting function beyond sexual activity, and positional pushing techniques or platelet-rich plasma lack evidence of benefit. (PMIDs 42686059, 41997518, 42771819, 42807524)
- **incretin-trials-lack-sex-disaggregated-data** — Cardiometabolic drug trials, biomarker cohorts and reviews report pooled efficacy and risk estimates without sex-disaggregated outcomes, even though female sex is the leading risk factor for GLP-1-associated hair loss. (PMIDs 42843337, 42875600, 42850058, 42933400, 42681850, 42709505)
- **first-trimester-blood-markers-obstetric-risk** — First-trimester markers predict obstetric risk only modestly and need local calibration: European preeclampsia algorithms misfit Chinese pregnancies and uterine artery Doppler varies by conception mode, though biomarker screening is cost-effective. (PMIDs 42723529, 42839342, 42919680, 42452901, 42383395, 42950944)
- **ivf-predictors-fail-prospective-testing** — Assisted-reproduction prognostic and genetic tests underperform on retesting: endometrial thickness fails to predict implantation, half of 'chaotic' niPGT-A embryos reclassify as normal, and first-cycle blastulation failure rarely repeats. (PMIDs 42926142, 42937235, 42966532)

## Emerging threads
- **endometriosis-detection-central-pain** — Transvaginal ultrasound detects ovarian endometriomas well but misses deep lesions; adding MRI raises sensitivity for rectosigmoid and uterosacral disease while lowering specificity, and central pain processing is altered in affected women. (PMIDs 42808571, 42922369, 42033133; first seen 2026-10-04)
- **bmi-misses-nutritional-risk** — BMI fails to identify malnutrition and muscle loss, which GLIM criteria, DXA/bioimpedance, mid-upper arm circumference and geriatric nutritional risk scores detect and link to amputation, hospitalization and mortality. (PMIDs 42956894, 42926060, 42475886, 42933604; first seen 2026-10-28)
- **sex-differences-in-drug-response** — Sex-stratified reanalyses reveal treatment effects concentrated in women, including davunetide in progressive supranuclear palsy and buprenorphine/naloxone's lower opioid toxicity risk, effects invisible in pooled reporting. (PMIDs 42399413, 42762708; first seen 2026-11-08)
- **first-trimester-fetal-brain-imaging-outpaces-interpretation** — First-trimester ultrasound can now visualize midline fetal brain structures such as the future cavum septi pellucidi, but interpretive standards for these immature structures lag behind the imaging capability. (PMIDs 42945420, 41808390; first seen 2026-11-03)
