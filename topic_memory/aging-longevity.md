# Topic Memory: Aging & Longevity
_Last updated: 2026-10-05 · source_id: aging-longevity · covers studies through 2026-10-04_

## Established findings
- **ai-biomarkers-marginal-cvd-risk** — AI methods and novel biomarkers added to cardiovascular assessment match expert measurement or yield small discrimination gains, with no demonstrated clinical benefit for patients. (PMIDs 42622203, 42648391, 42776068, 40780565)
- **single-arm-device-series-early-success** — Small retrospective or single-arm, often industry-funded series of new cardiovascular and metabolic devices report high technical success and few complications, but lack controls and long follow-up. (PMIDs 42556510, 42644273, 42757911, 42764110, 42331280)
- **biological-age-measure-discordance** — Quantitative aging measures disagree: clocks correlate weakly with each other, vary by organ and tissue, and respond inconsistently to exposures such as diet or alcohol. (PMIDs 42720847, 42417187, 42771094, 42791452, 42782071)
- **guideline-discordant-diagnosis-treatment** — Care for older adults departs from guidelines in both directions, with over-use alongside under-assessment, under-prescribing, and access barriers tied to sex, dementia label, and local regulation. (PMIDs 42570029, 42669184, 42723263, 42779225, 42754545)
- **family-caregiver-burden-older-adults** — Family caregivers and relatives of older adults carry heavy, often unaddressed emotional burden, feel sidelined in care conversations, and fare worse under intensive caregiving loads. (PMIDs 42648109, 41247831, 42685648)
- **eldercare-workforce-strain** — Institutional eldercare staff across India, China, the US, and Australia describe understaffing, training and language gaps, and burnout that drives turnover and constrains resident care. (PMIDs 42623904, 42643010, 42636502, 42202540)

## Emerging threads
- **composite-routine-blood-indices** — Composite indices and ratios derived from routine labs, including HALP and creatinine-to-cystatin C, stratify survival and cardiovascular events in older adults better than single conventional markers. (PMIDs 42776134; first seen 2026-08-24)
- **exercise-response-heterogeneity-older** — Response to structured exercise in older adults is highly heterogeneous, with baseline metabolic markers predicting gains and many participants failing to improve in expected domains. (PMIDs 42771097, 42790274; first seen 2026-09-24)
- **affective-symptoms-dementia-risk** — Anxiety in mild cognitive impairment raised dementia conversion risk by about a quarter, whereas depression after age 85 did not predict dementia, suggesting symptom- and age-specific risk. (PMIDs 42789462, 42791168; first seen 2026-09-26)
- **bone-quality-beyond-bmd** — Bone mineral density alone underestimates fracture risk: fracturing women showed altered collagen cross-linking, and DXA vertebral fracture assessment found silent fractures in about one in six adults. (PMIDs 42587084, 42624233; first seen 2026-08-13)
- **alzheimer-treatment-response-heterogeneity** — Brain-based factors, including locus coeruleus integrity and pre-existing brain frailty on MRI, are proposed to explain divergent cognitive and rehabilitation outcomes among older adults. (PMIDs 42611034, 42625098, 42777278; first seen 2026-08-21)
- **aortic-stenosis-flow-imaging-gaps** — Conventional echocardiography leaves aortic valve hemodynamics ambiguous, with flow-based criteria cutting indeterminate stress results and abnormal aortic flow persisting undetected after valve replacement. (PMIDs 42775464, 42770219; first seen 2026-09-29)
- **digital-monitoring-tools-older-adults** — Wearables, apps, and game-based assessments in older adults show good adherence and feasibility but so far add little demonstrated benefit over conventional counseling or clinic testing. (PMIDs 42789327, 42424747, 42759999; first seen 2026-09-29)
