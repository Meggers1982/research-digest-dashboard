# Topic Memory: Aging & Longevity
_Last updated: 2026-09-28 · source_id: aging-longevity · covers studies through 2026-09-27_

## Established findings
- **ai-biomarkers-marginal-cvd-risk** — Adding AI methods or novel biomarkers, including new ECG signals, to standard cardiovascular risk equations yields only small discrimination gains and no demonstrated clinical benefit. (PMIDs 42622203, 42648391, 42690761)
- **single-arm-device-series-early-success** — Small retrospective or single-arm series of new cardiovascular and metabolic devices report high technical success and few complications, but lack controls and long follow-up. (PMIDs 42556510, 42644273, 42757911, 42763030, 42764110, 42755127)
- **biological-age-measure-discordance** — Quantitative aging measures disagree: clocks correlate weakly with each other, vary by tissue, and show little independent response to dietary adherence in twins. (PMIDs 42720847, 42417187, 42771094, 42791452)
- **guideline-discordant-diagnosis-treatment** — Care for older adults departs from guidelines in both directions, with over-use alongside under-assessment, under-prescribing, and access barriers tied to sex, dementia label, and local regulation. (PMIDs 42570029, 42669184, 42723263, 42779225, 42754545)
- **family-caregiver-burden-older-adults** — Family caregivers and relatives of older adults carry heavy, often unaddressed emotional burden, feel sidelined in care conversations, and fare worse under intensive caregiving loads. (PMIDs 42648109, 41247831, 42685648)

## Emerging threads
- **composite-routine-blood-indices** — Composite indices and ratios from routine labs, including creatinine-to-cystatin C and HALP scores, outperform single conventional markers for cardiovascular events and survival in older adults. (PMIDs 42777518, 42754334; first seen 2026-08-24)
- **exercise-response-heterogeneity-older** — Structured exercise in adults over 65 produces only modest, inconsistent shifts in inflammatory biomarkers, and nearly half of older participants fail to improve in expected domains after resistance training. (PMIDs 42786581, 42771097; first seen 2026-09-24)
- **affective-symptoms-dementia-risk** — Anxiety in mild cognitive impairment raised dementia conversion risk by about a quarter, whereas depression after age 85 did not predict dementia, suggesting symptom- and age-specific risk. (PMIDs 42789462, 42791168; first seen 2026-09-26)
- **bone-quality-beyond-bmd** — Bone mineral density alone underestimates fracture risk: fracturing women showed altered collagen cross-linking, and DXA vertebral fracture assessment found silent fractures in about one in six adults. (PMIDs 42587084, 42624233; first seen 2026-08-13)
- **eldercare-workforce-strain** — Institutional eldercare staff across India, China, and the US describe understaffing, inadequate training, burnout driving turnover intention, and difficulty changing entrenched care routines. (PMIDs 42623904, 42643010, 42636502, 42760752; first seen 2026-08-24)
- **alzheimer-treatment-response-heterogeneity** — Brain-based factors, including locus coeruleus integrity and pre-existing brain frailty on MRI, are proposed to explain divergent cognitive and rehabilitation outcomes among older adults. (PMIDs 42611034, 42625098, 42777278; first seen 2026-08-21)
- **aging-theory-dysregulation** — Theoretical papers frame aging as directional dysregulation rather than random decay, invoking chronic DNA damage response overactivation and asymmetric costs of physiological regulation. (PMIDs 42684403, 42716445; first seen 2026-09-07)
