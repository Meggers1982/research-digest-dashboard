# Topic Memory: Aging & Longevity
_Last updated: 2026-09-14 · source_id: aging-longevity · covers studies through 2026-09-13_

## Established findings
- **ai-biomarkers-marginal-cvd-risk** — Adding AI methods or novel biomarkers, including new ECG signals, to standard cardiovascular risk equations yields only small discrimination gains and no demonstrated clinical benefit. (PMIDs 42622203, 42648391, 42690761)
- **single-arm-device-series-early-success** — Small retrospective or single-arm series of new cardiovascular devices and imaging techniques report high technical success and few complications, but lack controls and long follow-up. (PMIDs 42556510, 42584098, 42229558, 42644273, 42667242, 42702591)
- **contaminants-in-natural-products** — Chemical screening of widely consumed natural products, fungi, and human milk detects contaminants in nearly all samples, generally below regulatory limits, with mycelium less contaminated than fruiting bodies. (PMIDs 42572146, 42615889, 42641861, 42634105)
- **family-caregiver-burden-older-adults** — Family caregivers and relatives of older adults carry heavy, often unaddressed emotional burden, feel sidelined in care conversations, and fare worse under intensive caregiving loads. (PMIDs 42648109, 41247831, 42685648)
- **guideline-discordant-diagnosis-treatment** — Practice departs from guidelines in both directions: over-use and misapplied criteria in diagnosis, alongside under-prescribing, poor drug access, and falling adherence for recommended therapies in older adults. (PMIDs 42570029, 42475881, 42669184, 42723263, 42680576, 42702701)
- **noninvasive-signals-diabetes-complications** — Blood lipidomic panels and non-invasive signal models detect diabetic neuropathy or estimate glucose in single-center cohorts, but all await external validation. (PMIDs 42610662, 42618536, 42677686)

## Emerging threads
- **bone-quality-beyond-bmd** — Bone mineral density alone underestimates fracture risk: fracturing women showed altered collagen cross-linking, and DXA vertebral fracture assessment found silent fractures in about one in six adults. (PMIDs 42587084, 42624233; first seen 2026-08-13)
- **eldercare-workforce-strain** — Institutional eldercare staff across India, China, and the US describe understaffing, inadequate training, burnout driving turnover intention, and difficulty changing entrenched care routines. (PMIDs 42623904, 42643010, 42636502; first seen 2026-08-24)
- **alzheimer-treatment-response-heterogeneity** — Brain-based factors, including locus coeruleus integrity and connectivity matching, are proposed to explain why cognitive and anti-amyloid interventions help some older adults and not others. (PMIDs 42611034, 42625098; first seen 2026-08-21)
- **composite-routine-blood-indices** — Composite scores from routine labs, such as an insulin resistance plus hs-CRP index and the HALP score, outperformed single conventional markers for cardiovascular outcomes. (PMIDs 42681850, 42615786; first seen 2026-08-24)
- **aging-theory-dysregulation** — Two theoretical papers frame aging as directional dysregulation rather than random decay, invoking chronic DNA damage response overactivation and asymmetric costs of physiological regulation. (PMIDs 42684403, 42716445; first seen 2026-09-07)
- **biological-age-measure-discordance** — Competing quantitative aging and body-composition measures disagree with each other: senescence and epigenetic clocks correlate weakly, and AI fat-quantification tools give divergent values despite high precision. (PMIDs 42720847, 42417187; first seen 2026-09-08)
