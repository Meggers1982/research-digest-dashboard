# Topic Memory: Aging & Longevity
_Last updated: 2026-11-09 · source_id: aging-longevity · covers studies through 2026-11-08_

## Established findings
- **ai-biomarkers-marginal-cvd-risk** — AI methods and novel cardiovascular indices match expert measurement or yield small discrimination gains, with no demonstrated clinical benefit for patients. (PMIDs 42622203, 42648391, 42776068, 40780565, 42933922)
- **single-arm-device-series-early-success** — Small retrospective, first-in-human, or single-arm device studies, often industry-funded, report high technical success or accuracy but lack controls, outcomes, and long follow-up. (PMIDs 42556510, 42757911, 42913094, 42234368, 42965544)
- **biological-age-measure-discordance** — Quantitative aging measures disagree: clocks correlate weakly with each other, vary by organ and tissue, and respond inconsistently to exposures such as diet or alcohol. (PMIDs 42720847, 42417187, 42771094, 42791452, 42782071)
- **guideline-discordant-diagnosis-treatment** — Care for older adults departs from guidelines in both directions, with over-treatment that carries measurable harm alongside under-prescribing, withheld procedures, and access gaps by race, income, and age. (PMIDs 42833700, 42842171, 42844837, 42779225, 42754545)
- **eldercare-workforce-strain** — Eldercare staffing is constrained at both ends, with understaffing, training gaps, and burnout driving turnover and leaving frontline staff improvising dementia and mental health care. (PMIDs 42623904, 42643010, 42202540, 41172263, 41316828, 42967328)
- **family-caregiver-burden-older-adults** — Unpaid care of older adults falls disproportionately on women and leaves caregivers untrained and unsupported, with fatigue, isolation, anxiety, and financial strain reported across countries. (PMIDs 42610683, 41247831, 42958644, 41512003, 42468034, 42744296)
- **composite-routine-blood-indices** — Composite indices derived from routine labs and blood counts stratify cardiometabolic, biological aging, and survival risk at least as well as single conventional markers. (PMIDs 42776134, 42852842, 42853097, 42872815, 42681850)
- **digital-monitoring-tools-older-adults** — Digital, AI, and structured lifestyle programs for older adults show good adherence, accuracy, and acceptability but add no benefit on mortality, cognition, or biological aging measures. (PMIDs 42424747, 42759999, 42918505, 42950609, 42570533)
- **aortic-stenosis-flow-imaging-gaps** — Conventional echocardiography mischaracterizes valvular hemodynamics, overstating right ventricular function and identifying severe tricuspid regurgitation only after advanced heart failure appears. (PMIDs 42775464, 42849796, 42901428)

## Emerging threads
- **predictors-of-dementia-conversion** — Anxiety in mild cognitive impairment, CSF p62, and driving changes predict progression to dementia, while depression after 85 and Alzheimer's co-pathology in Parkinson's disease do not accelerate decline. (PMIDs 42789462, 42791168, 42961137, 42898004, 42975960; first seen 2026-09-26)
- **glp1-real-world-evidence-gaps** — Incretin drug practice outruns trial evidence, with database studies reporting unanticipated harms and benefits, uneven access, and newer multi-receptor agonists promoted on phase 1/2 data alone. (PMIDs 42873315, 42850058, 42886239, 39724554; first seen 2026-10-13)
- **fracture-risk-underdetection-older-adults** — Fragility fracture burden is missed by current thresholds and pathways: older men fracture as often as women despite lower measured osteoporosis, and liaison services cut refracture only after two years. (PMIDs 42965625, 42955080; first seen 2026-11-04)
