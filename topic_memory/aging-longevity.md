# Topic Memory: Aging & Longevity
_Last updated: 2026-11-02 · source_id: aging-longevity · covers studies through 2026-11-01_

## Established findings
- **ai-biomarkers-marginal-cvd-risk** — AI methods and novel cardiovascular indices match expert measurement or yield small discrimination gains, with no demonstrated clinical benefit for patients. (PMIDs 42622203, 42648391, 42776068, 40780565, 42933922)
- **single-arm-device-series-early-success** — Small retrospective, first-in-human, or single-arm device studies, often industry-funded, report high technical success or accuracy but lack controls, outcomes, and long follow-up. (PMIDs 42556510, 42757911, 42913094, 42933922, 42907139)
- **biological-age-measure-discordance** — Quantitative aging measures disagree: clocks correlate weakly with each other, vary by organ and tissue, and respond inconsistently to exposures such as diet or alcohol. (PMIDs 42720847, 42417187, 42771094, 42791452, 42782071)
- **guideline-discordant-diagnosis-treatment** — Care for older adults departs from guidelines in both directions, with over-treatment that carries measurable harm alongside under-prescribing, withheld procedures, and access gaps by race, income, and age. (PMIDs 42833700, 42842171, 42844837, 42779225, 42754545)
- **eldercare-workforce-strain** — Eldercare staffing is constrained at both ends, with understaffing, training gaps, and burnout driving turnover, prompting task-shifting programs that train nurses to deliver preventive geriatric visits. (PMIDs 42623904, 42643010, 42202540, 42852803, 42929476)
- **composite-routine-blood-indices** — Composite indices derived from routine labs and blood counts stratify cardiometabolic, biological aging, and survival risk at least as well as single conventional markers. (PMIDs 42776134, 42852842, 42853097, 42872815, 42906648)
- **digital-monitoring-tools-older-adults** — Digital, AI, and structured lifestyle programs for older adults show good adherence, accuracy, and acceptability but add no benefit on mortality, cognition, or biological aging measures. (PMIDs 42424747, 42759999, 42918505, 42950609, 42945122)
- **aortic-stenosis-flow-imaging-gaps** — Conventional echocardiography mischaracterizes valvular hemodynamics, overstating right ventricular function and identifying severe tricuspid regurgitation only after advanced heart failure appears. (PMIDs 42775464, 42849796, 42901428)

## Emerging threads
- **family-caregiver-burden-older-adults** — Unpaid care of older adults falls disproportionately on women, who enter caregiving roles about twice as often as men, and imbalance between giving and receiving support is proposed as an aging driver. (PMIDs 42610683, 41247831, 42958644; first seen 2026-09-05)
- **exercise-response-heterogeneity-older** — Response to structured exercise in older adults is highly heterogeneous, with baseline metabolic markers predicting gains and many participants failing to improve in expected domains. (PMIDs 42771097, 42790274; first seen 2026-09-24)
- **predictors-of-dementia-conversion** — Anxiety in mild cognitive impairment, CSF p62, and changes in naturalistic driving predict progression to dementia, while depression after age 85 does not; plasma Alzheimer's markers are stable enough for repeat testing. (PMIDs 42789462, 42791168, 42961137, 42898004, 42947225; first seen 2026-09-26)
- **glp1-real-world-evidence-gaps** — GLP-1 receptor agonist practice outruns trial evidence, with database studies reporting both unanticipated harms and benefits, very low certainty for off-label use, and uneven access. (PMIDs 42873315, 42850058, 42842171, 42886239; first seen 2026-10-13)
