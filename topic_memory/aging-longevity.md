# Topic Memory: Aging & Longevity
_Last updated: 2026-10-26 · source_id: aging-longevity · covers studies through 2026-10-25_

## Established findings
- **ai-biomarkers-marginal-cvd-risk** — AI methods and novel biomarkers added to cardiovascular assessment match expert measurement or yield small discrimination gains, with no demonstrated clinical benefit for patients. (PMIDs 42622203, 42648391, 42776068, 40780565)
- **single-arm-device-series-early-success** — Small retrospective, first-in-human, or single-arm device studies, often industry-funded, report high technical success and few complications but lack controls and long follow-up. (PMIDs 42556510, 42644273, 42757911, 42859373, 42872379)
- **biological-age-measure-discordance** — Quantitative aging measures disagree: clocks correlate weakly with each other, vary by organ and tissue, and respond inconsistently to exposures such as diet or alcohol. (PMIDs 42720847, 42417187, 42771094, 42791452, 42782071)
- **guideline-discordant-diagnosis-treatment** — Care for older adults departs from guidelines in both directions, with over-treatment that carries measurable harm alongside under-prescribing, withheld procedures, and access gaps by race, income, and age. (PMIDs 42833700, 42842171, 42844837, 42779225, 42754545)
- **eldercare-workforce-strain** — Eldercare staffing is constrained at both ends, with understaffing, training gaps, and burnout driving turnover while ageist attitudes deter trainees from geriatric careers. (PMIDs 42623904, 42643010, 42202540, 42852803)
- **composite-routine-blood-indices** — Composite indices and data-driven subtypes derived from routine labs, records, or glucose monitoring stratify cardiometabolic and survival risk at least as well as single conventional markers. (PMIDs 42776134, 42852842, 42853097, 42872815)
- **digital-monitoring-tools-older-adults** — Digital monitoring and structured lifestyle programs for older adults achieve good adherence and improve behaviors or intermediate markers, but add no benefit on mortality or biological aging measures. (PMIDs 42424747, 42759999, 42895589, 42918505)

## Emerging threads
- **family-caregiver-burden-older-adults** — Unpaid care of older adults falls disproportionately on family members, especially women, who enter caregiving roles about twice as often as men and carry largely unaddressed burden. (PMIDs 42610683, 41247831; first seen 2026-09-05)
- **exercise-response-heterogeneity-older** — Response to structured exercise in older adults is highly heterogeneous, with baseline metabolic markers predicting gains and many participants failing to improve in expected domains. (PMIDs 42771097, 42790274; first seen 2026-09-24)
- **affective-symptoms-dementia-risk** — Anxiety in mild cognitive impairment raised dementia conversion risk by about a quarter, whereas depression after age 85 did not predict dementia, suggesting symptom- and age-specific risk. (PMIDs 42789462, 42791168; first seen 2026-09-26)
- **aortic-stenosis-flow-imaging-gaps** — Conventional echocardiography mischaracterizes valvular hemodynamics, with flow-based criteria reducing indeterminate results and standard ejection fraction overstating right ventricular function in regurgitant disease. (PMIDs 42775464, 42770219, 42849796; first seen 2026-09-29)
- **glp1-real-world-evidence-gaps** — GLP-1 receptor agonist practice outruns trial evidence, with database studies reporting both unanticipated harms and benefits, very low certainty for off-label use, and uneven access. (PMIDs 42873315, 42850058, 42842171, 42886239; first seen 2026-10-13)
