# Topic Memory: Children's & Pediatric Health
_Last updated: 2026-10-05 · source_id: pediatric-health · covers studies through 2026-10-04_

## Established findings
- **single-center-biomarkers-pediatric-disease** — Small single-center pediatric studies keep proposing biomarkers, imaging indices, drug-level thresholds and bedside metrics to predict deterioration or disease course, with modest discrimination and no external validation. (PMIDs 42684427, 42670982, 42608005, 42665855, 42752806, 42763057, 42754724, 42786197)
- **guideline-discordant-pediatric-workup** — Actual pediatric practice diverges widely from recommended standards in investigation, imaging choice, preprocedure fasting, death review, child protection referral and time to diagnosis. (PMIDs 42135984, 42692799, 42776228, 42778190, 42820507)

## Emerging threads
- **adult-evidence-extrapolated-to-children** — Nutrition, exercise and exposure findings promoted as relevant to youth are tested almost entirely in adult samples with no pediatric subgroup, leaving child-specific effects and doses untested. (PMIDs 42788200, 42797090; first seen 2026-09-24)
- **pediatric-screening-tools-single-sample-validation** — New pediatric and caregiver assessment instruments report strong psychometrics from single narrow samples, and independent reexamination of established tools exposes measurement flaws. (PMIDs 42701327, 42788798, 42804916; first seen 2026-09-09)
- **early-life-nutrition-long-term-outcomes** — Exposures in pregnancy and the first 1,000 days — congenital infection, maternal physiology, feeding, caregiver language and the gut microbiome — track with children's later neurodevelopment, regulation and body composition. (PMIDs 42766296, 42706995, 42066677; first seen 2026-09-02)
- **chemical-contaminants-in-infant-child-diet** — Measurement studies repeatedly detect chemical contaminants in milk and children's staple foods, with infants and young children receiving the highest per-body-weight doses. (PMIDs 42716241; first seen 2026-09-02)
- **early-markers-predict-later-development** — Early neural and behavioral measures forecast later outcomes, including infant EEG predicting socioemotional problems, kindergarten attention predicting math skills, and modeled prediction of adolescent suicidal thoughts. (PMIDs 42671816, 42107140, 40681146; first seen 2026-09-05)
- **low-yield-routine-testing** — Routine escalation adds little: neuroimaging and EEG rarely explained first-episode pediatric psychosis, and empiric MRSA coverage in staphylococcal scalded skin syndrome lengthened stays without benefit. (PMIDs 42645338, 42702367; first seen 2026-09-02)
- **new-pediatric-therapies-monitoring-gap** — Specialist commentaries argue pediatric services lack surveillance structures for new therapies, including GLP-1 receptor agonists in childhood obesity and AAV gene therapy hepatotoxicity. (PMIDs 42692883, 42697506; first seen 2026-09-05)
- **implementation-barriers-child-services** — Program and provider reports place the bottleneck in implementation: resisted trauma-informed care, strained newborn screening, inconsistent breastfeeding education, unprepared epilepsy transition, and group well-child care that did not survive virtual delivery. (PMIDs 42641570, 42674592, 42674591, 42702196, 42790888, 42772671; first seen 2026-09-01)
- **individual-factors-adolescent-eating-disorders** — Beyond shared genetics, individual and cultural factors distinguish adolescents who develop disordered eating, with traditional food practices appearing protective in American Indian youth and generic exercise promotion possibly harmful. (PMIDs 42429700, 42683660, 42760826; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **feeding-tolerance-vulnerable-children** — Feeding management in fragile pediatric groups is being revisited: early enteral feeding was safe in growth-restricted preterm infants, and most children on long-term parenteral nutrition have unrecognized feeding difficulties. (PMIDs 42789183, 42785553; first seen 2026-09-28)
