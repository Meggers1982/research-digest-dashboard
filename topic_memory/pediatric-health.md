# Topic Memory: Children's & Pediatric Health
_Last updated: 2026-11-09 · source_id: pediatric-health · covers studies through 2026-11-08_

## Established findings
- **single-center-biomarkers-pediatric-disease** — Small single-center pediatric studies keep proposing biomarkers, imaging protocols, metabolic phenotypes and assessment tools with strong in-sample performance but no external validation. (PMIDs 42684427, 42786197, 42832973, 42492659)
- **guideline-discordant-pediatric-workup** — Delivered pediatric care diverges widely from recommended standards in investigation, imaging, death review, child protection referral, vaccination coverage and time to diagnosis. (PMIDs 42135984, 42692799, 42820507, 42853409, 42925553, 42911367)
- **adult-evidence-extrapolated-to-children** — Findings promoted as relevant to youth are tested in adult, mixed-age, conceptual or review samples with no pediatric subgroup analysis. (PMIDs 42841447, 42914757, 42944768, 42607453, 42166250, 42570404)
- **implementation-barriers-child-services** — Program, provider, caregiver and youth reports place the bottleneck in implementation: vague rules, stigma, staff resistance to new care models, language access and unprepared transition services. (PMIDs 42641570, 42674592, 42702196, 42790888, 42896296, 42912851, 42978031)

## Emerging threads
- **family-context-quality-over-quantity** — Relational context — partner support, care quality, bidirectional parent-youth dynamics — predicts child outcomes better than parental symptom levels or parenting style alone. (PMIDs 42932829, 42340381, 42816796; first seen 2026-10-28)
- **child-eating-behavior-resists-environment** — Children's appetitive traits look stable from early infancy and barely shift with home routine programs or school meal scheduling, which alter intake more than behavior. (PMIDs 42235608, 42951019, 42956672; first seen 2026-11-03)
- **newborn-first-hour-skin-to-skin** — The first hour of skin-to-skin contact shapes breastfeeding effectiveness, yet international guidance on preventing rare postnatal collapse during it conflicts sharply. (PMIDs 42934452, 42335534; first seen 2026-11-02)
- **caregiver-burden-chronic-complex-children** — Parents of children with chronic or medically complex conditions report fear of progression, anxiety exceeding their children's own, and unreported home safety workarounds. (PMIDs 42858961, 42842426, 42864277, 42875029, 42957180; first seen 2026-10-12)
- **early-life-nutrition-long-term-outcomes** — Perinatal and first-year exposures — congenital infection, maternal physiology, feeding, cumulative opioid dose — track with later neurodevelopmental and physical outcomes. (PMIDs 42766296, 42706995, 42892320, 42604745, 42946001; first seen 2026-09-02)
- **new-pediatric-therapies-monitoring-gap** — Pediatric services lack surveillance and outcome data for newer drugs and devices, from GLP-1 agonists and gene therapy to aerosolized surfactant and smart shunts. (PMIDs 42692883, 42697506, 42848058, 42442657, 42914136; first seen 2026-09-05)
- **individual-factors-adolescent-eating-disorders** — Individual, emotional and cultural factors distinguish youth who develop disordered eating, with emotional escape driving loss-of-control episodes and traditional food practices appearing protective. (PMIDs 42429700, 42683660, 42760826, 42387859; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **eating-disorder-recognition-gap** — Disordered eating is poorly captured by current pathways and research funding, with years of somatically coded visits before diagnosis and presentations fitting no diagnostic category. (PMIDs 42904521, 42925553, 42394263; first seen 2026-10-28)
- **low-yield-routine-testing** — Routine escalation adds little: neuroimaging and EEG rarely explained first-episode pediatric psychosis, empiric MRSA coverage lengthened stays, routine cardiac screening found normal hearts. (PMIDs 42645338, 42702367, 42855658; first seen 2026-09-02)
- **remote-delivered-pediatric-rehabilitation** — Remotely delivered, app- and tracker-supported care for youth shows high engagement and, in one randomized trial, faster recovery from persistent concussion symptoms. (PMIDs 42895728, 42933320; first seen 2026-10-24)
