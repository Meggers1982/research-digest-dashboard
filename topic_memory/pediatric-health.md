# Topic Memory: Children's & Pediatric Health
_Last updated: 2026-11-02 · source_id: pediatric-health · covers studies through 2026-11-01_

## Established findings
- **single-center-biomarkers-pediatric-disease** — Small single-center pediatric studies keep proposing biomarkers, imaging indices, metabolic phenotypes, monitoring metrics and assessment tools with strong in-sample performance but no external validation. (PMIDs 42684427, 42786197, 42867571, 42913737, 42933537, 42944567, 42961295)
- **guideline-discordant-pediatric-workup** — Delivered pediatric care diverges widely from recommended standards in investigation, imaging, death review, child protection referral, vaccination coverage and time to diagnosis. (PMIDs 42135984, 42692799, 42820507, 42853409, 42925553, 42911367)
- **adult-evidence-extrapolated-to-children** — Findings promoted as relevant to youth — nutrition, supplement formats, shopping behavior, gaming, learning, contaminant exposure — are tested in adult, conceptual or laboratory samples with no pediatric subgroup. (PMIDs 42841447, 42862435, 42914757, 42920261, 42937338, 42944768, 42607453, 42901119)
- **implementation-barriers-child-services** — Program, provider, caregiver and youth reports place the bottleneck in implementation: vague reporting rules, stigma, school culture, language access, strained screening and unprepared transition services. (PMIDs 42641570, 42674592, 42702196, 42790888, 42896296, 42905617, 42912851, 42922175)

## Emerging threads
- **caregiver-burden-chronic-complex-children** — Parents of children with chronic or medically complex conditions report high fear of progression, anxiety exceeding their children's own, traumatic treatment responsibility and unreported home safety workarounds. (PMIDs 42858961, 42842426, 42864277, 42875029, 42957180; first seen 2026-10-12)
- **adolescent-phone-use-depression-parenting** — In Chinese school self-report samples, depressive symptoms and harsh parenting are linked to problematic smartphone use and phone-mediated social withdrawal, with cognitive flexibility a candidate bridge. (PMIDs 42837874, 42861665; first seen 2026-10-12)
- **early-life-nutrition-long-term-outcomes** — Exposures in pregnancy and the first 1,000 days — congenital infection, maternal trauma and physiology, infant energy expenditure, feeding and the gut microbiome — track with later child outcomes. (PMIDs 42766296, 42706995, 42892320; first seen 2026-09-02)
- **new-pediatric-therapies-monitoring-gap** — Pediatric services lack surveillance for newer drugs and therapies, from GLP-1 receptor agonists and AAV gene therapy to neuropsychiatric reactions to second-generation antihistamines. (PMIDs 42692883, 42697506, 42848058; first seen 2026-09-05)
- **individual-factors-adolescent-eating-disorders** — Beyond shared genetics, individual and cultural factors distinguish adolescents who develop disordered eating, with traditional food practices appearing protective and generic exercise promotion possibly harmful. (PMIDs 42429700, 42683660, 42760826; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **eating-disorder-recognition-gap** — Disordered eating is poorly captured by current pathways, with years of somatically coded primary care visits before diagnosis and compensatory presentations fitting no existing diagnostic category. (PMIDs 42904521, 42925553; first seen 2026-10-28)
- **low-yield-routine-testing** — Routine escalation adds little: neuroimaging and EEG rarely explained first-episode pediatric psychosis, empiric MRSA coverage lengthened stays, and routine cardiac screening in X-linked ichthyosis found normal hearts. (PMIDs 42645338, 42702367, 42855658; first seen 2026-09-02)
- **remote-delivered-pediatric-rehabilitation** — Remotely delivered, app- and tracker-supported care for youth shows high engagement and, in one randomized trial, faster recovery from persistent concussion symptoms. (PMIDs 42895728, 42933320; first seen 2026-10-24)
- **family-context-quality-over-quantity** — Caregiver circumstances and care quality — employment, marital satisfaction, quality of grandparental involvement — predict child sleep, parenting trajectories and social development better than parenting style or care quantity. (PMIDs 42906210, 42932829, 42945004; first seen 2026-10-28)
