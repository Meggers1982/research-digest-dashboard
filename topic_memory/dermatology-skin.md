# Topic Memory: Dermatology & Skin Science
_Last updated: 2026-11-09 · source_id: dermatology-skin · covers studies through 2026-11-08_

## Established findings
- **benign-malignant-lesion-mimics** — Skin cancers and precancers are commonly misdiagnosed as benign inflammatory, eczematous or infectious lesions, especially at anogenital and nail sites and under autonomous AI triage, delaying diagnosis. (PMIDs 42340682, 42663047, 42850963, 42464899, 42969269, 42975407, 42685682)
- **skin-symptom-psychosocial-burden** — Symptomatic or visible skin disease carries measurable psychosocial, occupational and sleep burden for patients and for family caregivers, and screening tools are emerging to capture it. (PMIDs 42640063, 41964296, 42841656, 42947635, 42952132)
- **derm-treatment-adherence-barriers** — Dermatologic treatment satisfaction and adherence track patient experience—red tape, injection pain, unclear dosing, perceived relief—more than objective disease measures, and pain-reducing techniques help. (PMIDs 42693887, 42658203, 42863815, 42897084)

## Emerging threads
- **imaging-visual-assessment-reliability** — Unaided visual grading of skin disease is unreliable, particularly in pigmented skin, so standardized photography, dermoscopy, ultrasound, OCT-based AI and quantitative histology are being adopted as adjuncts. (PMIDs 42918724, 42946314, 42919815, 42929479, 42985976, 42198905, 42900687, 42710720, 42899449; first seen 2026-09-13)
- **advanced-therapy-uptake-gap** — Effective dermatologic therapies are under-used: steroids dominate in atopic dermatitis and alopecia areata, and most clinicians advise early vitiligo drug holidays for perceived non-response. (PMIDs 42928407, 42909837, 42842111, 42955645; first seen 2026-10-30)
- **fibrotic-skin-stromal-niche** — Spatial tissue mapping implicates distinct fibroblast and keratinocyte niches and mechanical tension signaling in keloids, hidradenitis, morphea and eosinophilic fasciitis. (PMIDs 42914005, 42297130; first seen 2026-10-24)
- **contact-allergen-exposure-patterns** — Consumer product allergens drive patch-test positivity—nickel jewelry, textile dyes, fragrances, topical corticosteroids—and sulfite-sensitized patients also report symptoms after eating high-sulfite foods. (PMIDs 42817757, 42817742, 42144702, 42913718, 42946549, 41040044; first seen 2026-10-02)
- **derm-care-standardization-consensus** — Delphi consensus panels are standardizing dermatologic care where trials are thin, covering hidradenitis units, scalp psoriasis, moderate atopic dermatitis and photodynamic therapy for actinic keratosis. (PMIDs 42869312, 42926054, 42909837, 42839189; first seen 2026-09-23)
- **ad-advanced-therapy-realworld** — Real-world data refine advanced eczema therapy: biomarkers track tralokinumab response, JAK inhibitors clear itch fastest but bring cutaneous viral infections, and dupilumab accrues lymphoma reports. (PMIDs 42700154, 42704201, 42825994, 42862144, 42685682; first seen 2026-09-09)
- **serum-proteomics-ctcl** — Proteomic signatures are being proposed to stage mycosis fungoides, predict progression and separate early cutaneous T-cell lymphoma from atopic dermatitis. (PMIDs 42934996, 42269854, 42685682; first seen 2026-10-30)
- **trial-representation-gaps** — Dermatologic evidence, assessment methods and terminology under-serve darker skin tones, Indigenous and non-English-speaking patients and low-data world regions, prompting global epidemiology initiatives. (PMIDs 42849744, 42847456, 42914005, 42918724, 42931730; first seen 2026-10-12)
- **derm-evidence-quality-concerns** — Evidence reaching dermatologists is shakier than assumed, with retractions, fragile network meta-analysis rankings, chatbot reasoning failures, numerical errors and inconsistent demographic reporting. (PMIDs 42776554, 42873516, 42849744; first seen 2026-09-08)
