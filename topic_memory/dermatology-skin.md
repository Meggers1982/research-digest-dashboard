# Topic Memory: Dermatology & Skin Science
_Last updated: 2026-10-23 · source_id: dermatology-skin · covers studies through 2026-10-18_

## Established findings
- **supervised-food-challenge-safety** — Supervised hospital food challenges and structured baked-food ladders rarely cause severe anaphylaxis, even in high-risk young children and when specific IgE levels are low. (PMIDs 42698161, 42663073, 42671940)
- **skin-symptom-psychosocial-burden** — Symptomatic or visible skin disease carries measurable psychosocial, occupational and sleep burden, from truncal acne stigma and uremic pruritus to job loss in ichthyosis. (PMIDs 42640063, 41964296, 42841656)
- **derm-treatment-adherence-barriers** — Nonadherence in dermatology stems from practical barriers—red tape, injection pain, unclear topical dosing—and targeted fixes such as vibration-cooling analgesia can ease them. (PMIDs 42693887, 42706904, 42658203, 42863815)

## Emerging threads
- **benign-malignant-lesion-mimics** — Skin cancers and precancers are commonly misdiagnosed as benign inflammatory or infectious lesions, with reassuring dermoscopy, PRAME staining and genital mimics all misdirecting clinicians. (PMIDs 42340682, 42663047, 42850963; first seen 2026-09-01)
- **derm-evidence-quality-concerns** — Evidence reaching dermatologists is shakier than assumed, with retractions, fragile network meta-analysis rankings, chatbot reasoning failures, numerical errors and inconsistent demographic reporting. (PMIDs 42776554, 42873516, 42849744; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **trial-representation-gaps** — Dermatology trial populations omit key groups, with children largely absent from alopecia areata trials and Indigenous Canadians nearly absent from atopic dermatitis evidence. (PMIDs 42849744, 42847456; first seen 2026-10-12)
- **ad-advanced-therapy-realworld** — Real-world data are refining advanced eczema therapy: blood biomarkers track tralokinumab response, JAK inhibitors clear itch fastest, and JAK use may bring cutaneous viral infections. (PMIDs 42700154, 42704201, 42825994, 42862144; first seen 2026-09-09)
- **contact-allergen-exposure-patterns** — Nickel remains the leading patch-test allergen and is still released by low-cost jewelry, while fragrances and preservatives drive underrecognized anogenital dermatitis in older women. (PMIDs 42817757, 42817742, 42144702; first seen 2026-10-02)
- **atopic-dermatitis-type2-continuum** — Atopic dermatitis is treated as part of a systemic type 2 process, shaping food challenge risk and prompting parasite screening before type 2-blocking biologics. (PMIDs 42336292, 42663073, 42712216; first seen 2026-09-04)
- **paradoxical-pustular-phenotype-switch** — Inflammatory skin phenotypes can switch under biologic therapy, with pustular psoriasis converting to eczematous disease and IL-17A blockade triggering paradoxical palmoplantar pustulosis. (PMIDs 42675660, 42730717; first seen 2026-09-07)
- **alopecia-diagnostic-adjuncts** — Hair loss assessment is moving beyond inspection, with Doppler ultrasound and scalp T-cell infiltration proposed as markers and metabolic syndrome linked to early-onset androgenetic alopecia. (PMIDs 42710720, 42702221, 42800559; first seen 2026-09-13)
- **derm-care-standardization-consensus** — Expert consensus and narrative reviews are standardizing dermatologic care where trial evidence is thin, covering hidradenitis suppurativa units, pseudofolliculitis barbae, and pregnancy management. (PMIDs 42762905, 42776558, 42768237, 42869312; first seen 2026-09-23)
