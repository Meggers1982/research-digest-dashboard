# Topic Memory: Dermatology & Skin Science
_Last updated: 2026-10-05 · source_id: dermatology-skin · covers studies through 2026-10-04_

## Established findings
- **supervised-food-challenge-safety** — Supervised hospital food challenges and structured baked-food ladders rarely cause severe anaphylaxis, even in high-risk young children and when specific IgE levels are low. (PMIDs 42698161, 42663073, 42671940)

## Emerging threads
- **benign-malignant-lesion-mimics** — Misdiagnosis of skin lesions is common and consequential, as reassuring dermoscopy, melanoma markers such as PRAME, and inflammatory mimics all misdirect diagnosis. (PMIDs 42340682, 42663047, 42765800, 42770911, 42499355; first seen 2026-09-01)
- **contact-allergen-exposure-patterns** — Nickel remains the leading patch-test allergen and is still released by low-cost jewelry, while fragrances and preservatives drive underrecognized anogenital dermatitis in older women. (PMIDs 42817757, 42817742, 42144702; first seen 2026-10-02)
- **derm-evidence-quality-concerns** — The evidence and decision aids reaching dermatologists are shakier than assumed, spanning paper mill retractions, fragile network meta-analysis rankings, chatbot reasoning failures, and overconfidence from visible disease. (PMIDs 42716351, 42242374, 42776554, 42777240, 42764811; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **derm-treatment-adherence-barriers** — Nonadherence and discontinuation in dermatology are driven by concrete practical barriers: administrative red tape, injection pain, unclear topical dosing methods, and lesion distribution such as truncal acne. (PMIDs 42693887, 42706904, 42658203; first seen 2026-09-01)
- **ad-advanced-therapy-realworld** — Real-world data are refining advanced eczema therapy: blood biomarkers track tralokinumab response, and JAK inhibitors clear itch and erythema faster than fissuring or other signs. (PMIDs 42700154, 42704201, 42825994; first seen 2026-09-09)
- **atopic-dermatitis-type2-continuum** — Atopic dermatitis is treated as part of a systemic type 2 process, shaping food challenge risk and prompting parasite screening before type 2-blocking biologics. (PMIDs 42336292, 42663073, 42712216; first seen 2026-09-04)
- **paradoxical-pustular-phenotype-switch** — Inflammatory skin phenotypes can switch under biologic therapy, with pustular psoriasis converting to eczematous disease and IL-17A blockade triggering paradoxical palmoplantar pustulosis. (PMIDs 42675660, 42730717; first seen 2026-09-07)
- **skin-symptom-psychosocial-burden** — Symptomatic or visible skin disease carries measurable psychosocial burden, including stigmatization in truncal acne, depression and poor sleep with uremic pruritus, and unmet clothing needs in lymphedema. (PMIDs 42640063, 41964296, 42768756; first seen 2026-09-01)
- **alopecia-diagnostic-adjuncts** — Hair loss assessment is moving beyond inspection, with Doppler ultrasound and scalp T-cell infiltration proposed as markers and metabolic syndrome linked to early-onset androgenetic alopecia. (PMIDs 42710720, 42702221, 42800559; first seen 2026-09-13)
- **derm-care-outside-dermatology** — Management of skin conditions increasingly happens outside dermatology, with non-dermatologists writing more topical ruxolitinib prescriptions and genital wart treatment varying by specialty and geography. (PMIDs 42761726, 42541273; first seen 2026-10-01)
- **gender-affirming-hair-removal-gaps** — Unwanted facial hair in transfeminine adults is rarely formally diagnosed and hard to treat, prompting arguments to reclassify permanent hair removal as medically necessary care. (PMIDs 42776550, 42776519; first seen 2026-09-26)
- **derm-care-standardization-consensus** — Expert consensus and clinical reviews are standardizing care where trial evidence is thin, covering hidradenitis suppurativa unit certification, pseudofolliculitis barbae, and HS management across pregnancy. (PMIDs 42762905, 42776558, 42768237; first seen 2026-09-23)
