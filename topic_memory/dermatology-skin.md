# Topic Memory: Dermatology & Skin Science
_Last updated: 2026-09-28 · source_id: dermatology-skin · covers studies through 2026-09-27_

## Established findings
- **supervised-food-challenge-safety** — Supervised hospital food challenges and structured baked-food ladders rarely cause severe anaphylaxis, even in high-risk young children and when specific IgE levels are low. (PMIDs 42698161, 42663073, 42671940)

## Emerging threads
- **benign-malignant-lesion-mimics** — Misdiagnosis of skin lesions and eruptions is common and consequential, with benign, previously treated, and paraneoplastic lesions mimicking malignancy or common dermatoses. (PMIDs 42340682, 42663047, 42748471, 42765800; first seen 2026-09-01)
- **derm-evidence-quality-concerns** — The evidence and decision aids reaching dermatologists are shakier than assumed, spanning paper mill retractions, fragile network meta-analysis rankings, and chatbots that fail harder reasoning tasks. (PMIDs 42716351, 42242374, 42776554, 42777240; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **derm-treatment-adherence-barriers** — Nonadherence and discontinuation in dermatology are driven by concrete practical barriers: administrative red tape, injection pain, unclear topical dosing methods, and lesion distribution such as truncal acne. (PMIDs 42693887, 42706904, 42658203; first seen 2026-09-01)
- **ad-advanced-therapy-realworld** — Real-world data are refining advanced atopic dermatitis therapy: routine blood biomarkers track tralokinumab response, and JAK inhibitors differ in speed of itch relief despite similar clearance. (PMIDs 42700154, 42704201; first seen 2026-09-09)
- **atopic-dermatitis-type2-continuum** — Atopic dermatitis is treated as part of a systemic type 2 process, shaping food challenge risk and prompting parasite screening before type 2-blocking biologics. (PMIDs 42336292, 42663073, 42712216; first seen 2026-09-04)
- **paradoxical-pustular-phenotype-switch** — Inflammatory skin phenotypes can switch under biologic therapy, with pustular psoriasis converting to eczematous disease and IL-17A blockade triggering paradoxical palmoplantar pustulosis. (PMIDs 42675660, 42730717; first seen 2026-09-07)
- **skin-symptom-psychosocial-burden** — Symptomatic or visible skin disease carries measurable psychosocial burden, including stigmatization in truncal acne and depression, poor sleep, and lower cognition with uremic pruritus. (PMIDs 42640063, 41964296; first seen 2026-09-01)
- **alopecia-diagnostic-adjuncts** — Hair loss diagnosis is moving beyond clinical inspection, with high-resolution Doppler ultrasound proposed for alopecia subtyping and scalp T-cell infiltration proposed as a female pattern hair loss marker. (PMIDs 42710720, 42702221; first seen 2026-09-13)
- **uv-exposure-harm-reassessment** — Estimates of ultraviolet and solar exposure harm are being revised, with lower-than-assumed skin cancer incidence after psoralen-free phototherapy and ambient solar radiation linked to incident presbyopia. (PMIDs 42341930, 41685990; first seen 2026-09-02)
- **gender-affirming-hair-removal-gaps** — Unwanted facial hair in transfeminine adults is rarely formally diagnosed and hard to treat, prompting arguments to reclassify permanent hair removal as medically necessary care. (PMIDs 42776550, 42776519; first seen 2026-09-26)
- **derm-care-standardization-consensus** — Expert Delphi consensus is being used to standardize care where trial evidence is thin, covering hidradenitis suppurativa unit certification and pseudofolliculitis barbae management. (PMIDs 42762905, 42776558; first seen 2026-09-23)
