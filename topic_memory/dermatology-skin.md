# Topic Memory: Dermatology & Skin Science
_Last updated: 2026-11-02 · source_id: dermatology-skin · covers studies through 2026-11-01_

## Established findings
- **supervised-food-challenge-safety** — Supervised hospital food challenges and structured baked-food ladders rarely cause severe anaphylaxis, even in high-risk young children and when specific IgE levels are low. (PMIDs 42698161, 42663073, 42671940)
- **skin-symptom-psychosocial-burden** — Symptomatic or visible skin disease carries measurable psychosocial, occupational and sleep burden, and screening tools are now being built to capture this mental-health component in clinic. (PMIDs 42640063, 41964296, 42841656, 42947635)
- **derm-treatment-adherence-barriers** — Dermatologic treatment satisfaction and adherence track patient experience—red tape, injection pain, unclear dosing, perceived symptom relief—more than objective disease measures, and pain-reducing techniques help. (PMIDs 42693887, 42658203, 42863815, 42897084)

## Emerging threads
- **benign-malignant-lesion-mimics** — Skin cancers and precancers are commonly missed or misdiagnosed as benign inflammatory or infectious lesions, including by autonomous AI triage and at anogenital sites, where diagnosis comes late. (PMIDs 42340682, 42663047, 42850963, 42464899; first seen 2026-09-01)
- **trial-representation-gaps** — Dermatologic evidence, assessment methods and terminology under-serve darker skin tones, Indigenous and non-English-speaking patients, and low-data world regions, prompting global epidemiology initiatives. (PMIDs 42849744, 42847456, 42914005, 42918724, 42931730; first seen 2026-10-12)
- **derm-care-standardization-consensus** — Delphi consensus panels are standardizing dermatologic care where trial evidence is thin, now covering hidradenitis units, scalp psoriasis formulations and the classification of moderate atopic dermatitis. (PMIDs 42869312, 42926054, 42909837; first seen 2026-09-23)
- **derm-evidence-quality-concerns** — Evidence reaching dermatologists is shakier than assumed, with retractions, fragile network meta-analysis rankings, chatbot reasoning failures, numerical errors and inconsistent demographic reporting. (PMIDs 42776554, 42873516, 42849744; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **ad-advanced-therapy-realworld** — Real-world data are refining advanced eczema therapy: blood biomarkers track tralokinumab response, JAK inhibitors clear itch fastest, and JAK use may bring cutaneous viral infections. (PMIDs 42700154, 42704201, 42825994, 42862144; first seen 2026-09-09)
- **advanced-therapy-uptake-gap** — Despite approved targeted drugs, most patients with atopic dermatitis or severe alopecia areata still receive corticosteroids, leaving moderate disease undertreated and satisfaction low. (PMIDs 42928407, 42909837, 42842111; first seen 2026-10-30)
- **imaging-visual-assessment-reliability** — Unaided visual grading of skin disease shows poor interrater reliability and fails in deeply pigmented skin, driving adoption of standardized photography, dermoscopy and noninvasive imaging. (PMIDs 42918724, 42946314, 42919815, 42929479; first seen 2026-10-26)
- **contact-allergen-exposure-patterns** — Consumer product allergens keep driving patch-test positivity, with nickel from low-cost jewelry, rising textile dye reactions in Italy, fragrances in anogenital dermatitis, and topical corticosteroids themselves. (PMIDs 42817757, 42817742, 42144702, 42913718, 42946549; first seen 2026-10-02)
- **alopecia-diagnostic-adjuncts** — Hair loss assessment is moving beyond inspection, with Doppler ultrasound, scalp T-cell infiltration and new histological markers separating traction alopecia from trichotillomania. (PMIDs 42710720, 42702221, 42800559, 42899449; first seen 2026-09-13)
- **fibrotic-skin-stromal-niche** — Tissue-level mapping implicates distinct fibroblast and keratinocyte niches, plus mechanical tension signaling, in treatment-resistant fibrotic and scarring skin disease such as keloids and hidradenitis suppurativa. (PMIDs 42504714, 42914005; first seen 2026-10-24)
- **serum-proteomics-ctcl** — Serum proteomic signatures are being proposed to stage mycosis fungoides, predict progression, and separate early cutaneous T-cell lymphoma from atopic dermatitis. (PMIDs 42934996, 42269854; first seen 2026-10-30)
