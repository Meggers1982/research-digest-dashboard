# Topic Memory: Fitness & Exercise
_Last updated: 2026-10-26 · source_id: fitness-exercise · covers studies through 2026-10-25_

## Established findings
- **slower-return-to-sport-timelines** — Athletes cleared to return to sport after ACL or hamstring injury retain measurable neuromuscular and functional deficits, and slower timelines are associated with better outcomes without performance cost. (PMIDs 42044292, 42419036, 41934354, 42676084, 42781749)
- **perceptual-gains-without-physical-gains** — Ergogenic, priming and technology add-ons change perception, confidence or vitality while measured physical performance and function remain unchanged relative to the base program. (PMIDs 42677822, 41818458, 42781727, 42785717, 42852832, 42899516)
- **male-derived-guidance-sex-gaps** — Male-derived guidance, thresholds and equipment fit female athletes poorly: standard caffeine doses give no benefit, men's-cut footwear raises injury odds, and velocity-loss cutoffs underestimate women's effort. (PMIDs 42770512, 42858003, 42841490, 42926124)

## Emerging threads
- **measurement-validity-is-context-dependent** — Common tests, scans and load metrics misrepresent athlete status, with retest noise, silent pathology and monitoring indices that fail to track balance or next-day neuromuscular performance. (PMIDs 42700344, 42711864, 42687355, 42798771, 42862018, 42812011, 42302193; first seen 2026-09-04)
- **exercise-safe-in-cautioned-populations** — Vigorous, high-impact or assisted exercise is safe and effective in groups usually advised to hold back, including hip osteoarthritis, twin pregnancy, non-ambulant cerebral palsy and adolescent patellofemoral pain. (PMIDs 42414085, 42754383, 42873200, 42895359; first seen 2026-09-24)
- **mvpa-shortfall-despite-supportive-settings** — Recommended activity levels and fitness gains go unmet even in monitored or supportive contexts, including school multisport programs, inpatient care and older adults with chronic conditions. (PMIDs 42700754, 42671694, 41533689, 42785145, 42785724; first seen 2026-09-02)
- **recovery-and-psych-state-predict-injury** — Psychological and perceptual state predicts injury risk, functional self-appraisal and return to sport better than physical capacity, though pre-existing mental health diagnoses do not worsen post-surgical knee outcomes. (PMIDs 42687223, 41388586, 42790762, 41992280, 42803416, 42852817, 42830691; first seen 2026-09-03)
- **access-barriers-limit-rehab-participation** — Continued rehabilitation is constrained by program capacity, cost, absent follow-up, unmet psychological needs and inequitable diagnosis rather than patient motivation. (PMIDs 42803416, 42303535, 42790519, 42906889, 42900173; first seen 2026-09-28)
- **protein-dose-above-adequacy** — Once intake is adequate, extra protein, amino acid or very high carbohydrate loading yields only marginal or mechanistically unexplained performance and recovery benefits. (PMIDs 42248424, 42696639, 42700960, 42258036; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **first-contact-physiotherapy** — Rehabilitation professionals are being placed at the point of first musculoskeletal contact, in general practice and joint surgeon-therapist consultations, and are rated well prepared for the role. (PMIDs 42644384, 41895244, 42782070; first seen 2026-09-02)
- **athlete-support-expertise-gaps** — People surrounding elite athletes are untrained or excluded: team clinicians lack cannabis education, pharmacists' anti-doping expertise goes unused, and parents feel outside formal support structures. (PMIDs 42861892, 42852660, 42874947; first seen 2026-10-14)
- **sleep-duration-and-season-load** — In elite female athletes, longer sleep duration rather than sleep quality tracks faster race times, and REM sleep declines once the competitive season begins. (PMIDs 42911853, 42906892; first seen 2026-10-25)
