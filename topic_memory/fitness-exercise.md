# Topic Memory: Fitness & Exercise
_Last updated: 2026-11-09 · source_id: fitness-exercise · covers studies through 2026-11-08_

## Established findings
- **slower-return-to-sport-timelines** — Athletes cleared after knee, ACL or tendon injury retain deficits, with inflammatory, cartilage and neural abnormalities persisting a year or more after rehabilitation. (PMIDs 42044292, 42419036, 41934354, 42676084, 42781749, 42949220, 42929659, 42975142, 42935279)
- **perceptual-gains-without-physical-gains** — Ergogenic, educational and technology add-ons shift perception, confidence, fear or vitality while measured physical performance and tissue healing remain unchanged relative to the base program. (PMIDs 42677822, 41818458, 42781727, 42785717, 42852832, 42899516, 42363428)
- **male-derived-guidance-sex-gaps** — Male-derived guidance, thresholds and equipment fit female athletes poorly, and sex-specific morphology, mechanics and menstrual-cycle effects remain poorly understood by players and staff. (PMIDs 42770512, 42858003, 42841490, 42926124, 42954471, 42975565, 41729514)
- **recovery-and-psych-state-predict-injury** — Self-reported pain, sleep, stress and psychological state predict injury and return to sport better than physical capacity, and minimizing symptoms during recovery worsens mental health outcomes. (PMIDs 42687223, 41388586, 42790762, 41992280, 42803416, 42852817, 42945849, 42934181)

## Emerging threads
- **measurement-validity-is-context-dependent** — Common tests, scans and load metrics misrepresent individual status, with static strength failing to predict movement and gait or balance models classifying disease worse than alternative sensors. (PMIDs 42700344, 42711864, 42687355, 42798771, 42862018, 42931089, 42947266, 42911838, 42949202, 42435928, 42249786; first seen 2026-09-04)
- **exercise-safe-in-cautioned-populations** — Vigorous, intensive or assisted exercise is safe and effective in groups usually advised to hold back, including hip osteoarthritis, twin pregnancy, cerebral palsy and adolescent patellofemoral pain. (PMIDs 42414085, 42754383, 42873200, 42895359, 42530921; first seen 2026-09-24)
- **mvpa-shortfall-despite-supportive-settings** — Recommended activity and limb use go unmet even in monitored settings, with hospitalized older adults inactive half the day and stroke survivors using the affected arm far less at home than in therapy. (PMIDs 42700754, 42671694, 41533689, 42785145, 42950801, 42318890; first seen 2026-09-02)
- **access-barriers-limit-rehab-participation** — Continued rehabilitation is constrained by program capacity, cost, absent follow-up and inequitable access, and the supporting trial evidence itself largely excludes rural participants. (PMIDs 42803416, 42303535, 42790519, 42906889, 42900173, 42384581; first seen 2026-09-28)
- **protein-dose-above-adequacy** — Once basic intake is adequate, extra protein, amino acids and specialty rehydration drinks add little beyond ordinary food and plain water. (PMIDs 42248424, 42696639, 42700960, 42258036, 42601056; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **athlete-support-expertise-gaps** — People surrounding elite athletes lack relevant expertise, from cannabinoid knowledge to menstrual-cycle physiology, while pharmacist and parent knowledge goes unused. (PMIDs 42861892, 42852660, 42874947, 42902901, 41729514; first seen 2026-10-14)
- **sleep-duration-and-season-load** — Longer sleep duration rather than quality tracks athlete performance, yet duration resists instruction and pre-bed protein, and cold exposure near bedtime may impair sleep onset. (PMIDs 42911853, 42906892, 42949008, 42897977, 42934391; first seen 2026-10-25)
- **pain-severity-drives-movement-and-disability** — Pain intensity and pain-related fear, not structural findings or measured capacity, explain altered movement strategies, reduced muscle activation and functional disability. (PMIDs 42914580, 42950810, 42960970, 42935325; first seen 2026-10-26)
- **passive-add-ons-no-advantage-over-simpler-care** — For musculoskeletal pain, the particular passive device, injection or immobilization chosen adds no pain benefit over simpler options paired with exercise or activity modification. (PMIDs 42935354, 42948885, 42928080; first seen 2026-10-30)
- **adapted-remote-delivery-for-disability** — Remote and home-delivered exercise and rehabilitation are acceptable to users, often preferred over in-person care, and report gains in activity and function in disabled and chronic-pain groups. (PMIDs 42904567, 42921738, 42954306, 42904907, 42900630, 42966905, 42435786; first seen 2026-10-26)
