# Topic Memory: Fitness & Exercise
_Last updated: 2026-10-05 · source_id: fitness-exercise · covers studies through 2026-10-04_

## Established findings
- **slower-return-to-sport-timelines** — Athletes cleared to return to sport after ACL or hamstring injury retain measurable neuromuscular and functional deficits, and slower timelines are associated with better outcomes without performance cost. (PMIDs 42044292, 42419036, 41934354, 42676084, 42781749)
- **perceptual-gains-without-physical-gains** — Acute ergogenic and priming strategies such as carbohydrate mouth rinsing, hot-water immersion and virtual reality alter perception or cognition but leave measured physical performance unchanged. (PMIDs 42677822, 41818458, 42781727, 42785717)

## Emerging threads
- **measurement-validity-is-context-dependent** — Common tests, scales and single indices gauge surrogates rather than the intended construct, missing pathology and functional limitation that targeted phenotyping or purpose-built tools reveal. (PMIDs 42700344, 42711864, 42687355, 42798771, 42786426; first seen 2026-09-04)
- **male-derived-guidance-sex-gaps** — Guidance and equipment derived from male athletes fit females poorly, with different fatigue mechanisms, no benefit from higher carbohydrate targets, and higher injury odds in men's-cut footwear. (PMIDs 42770512; first seen 2026-09-01)
- **mvpa-shortfall-despite-supportive-settings** — Recommended activity levels and fitness gains go unmet even in monitored or supportive contexts, including school multisport programs, inpatient care and older adults with chronic conditions. (PMIDs 42700754, 42671694, 41533689, 42785145, 42785724; first seen 2026-09-02)
- **recovery-and-psych-state-predict-injury** — Psychological and perceptual state — stress, sleep, mood, perceived health and mental fatigue — predicts injury risk, functional self-appraisal and activity choices better than training load or physical capacity. (PMIDs 42687223, 41388586, 42790762, 41992280, 42803416; first seen 2026-09-03)
- **access-barriers-limit-rehab-participation** — Continued rehabilitation and activity after acute care are constrained by program capacity, cost, equipment servicing and absent follow-up rather than patient motivation. (PMIDs 42803416, 42790273, 42303535, 42790519; first seen 2026-09-28)
- **exercise-safe-in-cautioned-populations** — Vigorous, high-impact or assisted exercise appears safe and feasible in groups usually advised to hold back, including hip osteoarthritis, twin pregnancy and non-ambulant cerebral palsy. (PMIDs 42414085, 42754383, 42768904; first seen 2026-09-24)
- **protein-dose-above-adequacy** — Once intake is adequate, extra protein, amino acid or very high carbohydrate loading yields only marginal or mechanistically unexplained performance and recovery benefits. (PMIDs 42248424, 42696639, 42700960, 42258036; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **priming-before-skill-practice** — Acute aerobic exercise improved motor learning only when performed before skill practice, and practitioner frameworks propose similar preconditioning logic for performance enhancement. (PMIDs 42692380, 42136134; first seen 2026-09-02)
- **first-contact-physiotherapy** — Rehabilitation professionals are being placed at the point of first musculoskeletal contact, in general practice and joint surgeon-therapist consultations, and are rated well prepared for the role. (PMIDs 42644384, 41895244, 42782070; first seen 2026-09-02)
- **concussion-burden-and-early-markers** — Adolescent sport-related concussion is linked to lower quality of life a decade later, and clinic exercise intolerance testing doubles the detected odds of prolonged recovery. (PMIDs 42696730, 42703626; first seen 2026-09-09)
