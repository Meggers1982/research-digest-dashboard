# Topic Memory: Fitness & Exercise
_Last updated: 2026-09-14 · source_id: fitness-exercise · covers studies through 2026-09-13_

## Established findings
- **slower-return-to-sport-timelines** — Athletes cleared to return to sport after ACL or hamstring injury retain measurable neuromuscular and functional deficits, and slower timelines are associated with better outcomes without performance cost. (PMIDs 42044292, 42419036, 41934354, 42676084)

## Emerging threads
- **measurement-validity-is-context-dependent** — Common tests and exercises deliver on their claims only in narrow conditions, and often gauge or load surrogates rather than the intended target construct or muscle. (PMIDs 42700344, 42711864, 42687355, 42501948, 42199094, 42639742; first seen 2026-09-04)
- **male-derived-guidance-sex-gaps** — Fueling and ergogenic guidance rests largely on male samples, and female-specific studies show different fatigue mechanisms, side-effect profiles, and no benefit from higher carbohydrate targets. (PMIDs 42704774, 42700960, 42521196; first seen 2026-09-01)
- **mvpa-shortfall-despite-supportive-settings** — Recommended activity levels go unmet even in monitored or supportive contexts, from epilepsy inpatients to older adults with HIV and pupils in whole-school activity programs. (PMIDs 42700754, 42671694, 41533689; first seen 2026-09-02)
- **recovery-and-psych-state-predict-injury** — In competitive athletes, poor recovery status and pre-race psychological traits such as stress and sleep quality predict injury better than training load. (PMIDs 42687223, 41388586; first seen 2026-09-03)
- **perceptual-gains-without-physical-gains** — Acute interventions improved perception, enjoyment, or cognition without physical benefit, as with carbohydrate mouth rinsing in soccer decision-making and virtual reality rowing. (PMIDs 42677822, 41818458; first seen 2026-09-03)
- **priming-before-skill-practice** — Acute aerobic exercise improved motor learning only when performed before skill practice, and practitioner frameworks propose similar preconditioning logic for performance enhancement. (PMIDs 42692380, 42136134; first seen 2026-09-02)
- **first-contact-physiotherapy** — Physiotherapists are rated best prepared as first-contact musculoskeletal providers and are being embedded in general practice across several countries. (PMIDs 42644384, 41895244; first seen 2026-09-02)
- **protein-dose-above-adequacy** — Once protein and energy intake are adequate, extra protein source, amino acid, or carbohydrate loading gives only marginal or outcome-specific recovery and adaptation benefits. (PMIDs 42248424, 42696639, 42700960; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **concussion-burden-and-early-markers** — Adolescent sport-related concussion is linked to lower quality of life a decade later, and clinic exercise intolerance testing doubles the detected odds of prolonged recovery. (PMIDs 42696730, 42703626; first seen 2026-09-09)
