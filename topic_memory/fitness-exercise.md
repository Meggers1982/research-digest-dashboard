# Topic Memory: Fitness & Exercise
_Last updated: 2026-11-02 · source_id: fitness-exercise · covers studies through 2026-11-01_

## Established findings
- **slower-return-to-sport-timelines** — Athletes cleared to return to sport after knee, ACL or tendon problems retain deficits, and slow return often reflects condition severity rather than the procedure, with slower timelines not costing performance. (PMIDs 42044292, 42419036, 41934354, 42676084, 42781749, 42949220)
- **perceptual-gains-without-physical-gains** — Ergogenic, priming and technology add-ons change perception, confidence or vitality while measured physical performance and function remain unchanged relative to the base program. (PMIDs 42677822, 41818458, 42781727, 42785717, 42852832, 42899516)
- **male-derived-guidance-sex-gaps** — Male-derived guidance, thresholds and equipment fit female athletes poorly: standard caffeine doses give no benefit, men's-cut footwear raises injury odds, and velocity-loss cutoffs underestimate women's effort. (PMIDs 42770512, 42858003, 42841490, 42926124)
- **recovery-and-psych-state-predict-injury** — Self-reported pain, sleep, stress and psychological state predict injury and return to sport better than physical capacity, and minimizing symptoms during recovery worsens mental health outcomes. (PMIDs 42687223, 41388586, 42790762, 41992280, 42803416, 42852817, 42945849, 42934181)

## Emerging threads
- **measurement-validity-is-context-dependent** — Common tests, scans, screeners and load metrics misrepresent individual status, with poor retest reliability, silent pathology, protocol-dependent profiles and indices that fail to predict the outcome they are assumed to measure. (PMIDs 42700344, 42711864, 42687355, 42798771, 42862018, 42931089, 42947266, 42911838, 42949202; first seen 2026-09-04)
- **exercise-safe-in-cautioned-populations** — Vigorous, high-impact or assisted exercise is safe and effective in groups usually advised to hold back, including hip osteoarthritis, twin pregnancy, non-ambulant cerebral palsy and adolescent patellofemoral pain. (PMIDs 42414085, 42754383, 42873200, 42895359; first seen 2026-09-24)
- **mvpa-shortfall-despite-supportive-settings** — Recommended activity levels go unmet even in monitored or supportive contexts, with hospitalized older adults inactive for half the day and school and inpatient programs falling short. (PMIDs 42700754, 42671694, 41533689, 42785145, 42950801; first seen 2026-09-02)
- **access-barriers-limit-rehab-participation** — Continued rehabilitation is constrained by program capacity, cost, absent follow-up, unmet psychological needs and inequitable diagnosis rather than patient motivation. (PMIDs 42803416, 42303535, 42790519, 42906889, 42900173; first seen 2026-09-28)
- **protein-dose-above-adequacy** — Once intake is adequate, extra protein, amino acid or very high carbohydrate loading yields only marginal or mechanistically unexplained performance and recovery benefits. (PMIDs 42248424, 42696639, 42700960, 42258036; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **athlete-support-expertise-gaps** — People surrounding elite athletes lack relevant expertise: cannabinoid use is common while athletes and medical staff know little about it, and pharmacist and parent knowledge goes unused. (PMIDs 42861892, 42852660, 42874947, 42902901; first seen 2026-10-14)
- **sleep-duration-and-season-load** — Longer sleep duration rather than quality tracks athlete performance, yet duration resists instruction and pre-bed protein, and cold exposure near bedtime may impair sleep onset. (PMIDs 42911853, 42906892, 42949008, 42897977, 42934391; first seen 2026-10-25)
- **passive-add-ons-no-advantage-over-simpler-care** — For musculoskeletal pain, the particular passive device, injection or immobilization chosen adds no pain benefit over simpler options paired with exercise or activity modification. (PMIDs 42935354, 42948885, 42928080; first seen 2026-10-30)
- **choice-architecture-beats-individual-messaging** — Taxes and default healthier swaps change purchasing behavior, while individual cognitive training and moderation messaging leave food choices unchanged. (PMIDs 42929274, 42937338, 42913077; first seen 2026-10-28)
- **pain-severity-drives-movement-and-disability** — Pain intensity and pain-related fear, not structural findings or measured capacity, explain altered movement strategies, reduced muscle activation and functional disability. (PMIDs 42914580, 42950810, 42960970, 42935325; first seen 2026-10-26)
- **adapted-remote-delivery-for-disability** — Small uncontrolled programs delivered online, at home or through play report increased activity and function in wheelchair users and children with disabilities, with users shaping the designs. (PMIDs 42904567, 42921738, 42954306, 42904907, 42900630; first seen 2026-10-26)
