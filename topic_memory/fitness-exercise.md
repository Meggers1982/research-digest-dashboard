# Topic Memory: Fitness & Exercise
_Last updated: 2026-09-28 · source_id: fitness-exercise · covers studies through 2026-09-27_

## Established findings
- **slower-return-to-sport-timelines** — Athletes cleared to return to sport after ACL or hamstring injury retain measurable neuromuscular and functional deficits, and slower timelines are associated with better outcomes without performance cost. (PMIDs 42044292, 42419036, 41934354, 42676084)
- **perceptual-gains-without-physical-gains** — Acute ergogenic and priming strategies such as carbohydrate mouth rinsing, hot-water immersion and virtual reality alter perception or cognition but leave measured physical performance unchanged. (PMIDs 42677822, 41818458, 42781727, 42785717)

## Emerging threads
- **measurement-validity-is-context-dependent** — Common tests, scales and indices deliver on their claims only in narrow conditions, often gauging surrogates rather than the intended construct, muscle or clinical decision. (PMIDs 42700344, 42711864, 42687355, 42759303, 42798206, 42762156; first seen 2026-09-04)
- **male-derived-guidance-sex-gaps** — Guidance and equipment derived from male athletes fit females poorly, with different fatigue mechanisms, no benefit from higher carbohydrate targets, and higher injury odds in men's-cut footwear. (PMIDs 42770512; first seen 2026-09-01)
- **mvpa-shortfall-despite-supportive-settings** — Recommended activity levels go unmet even in monitored or supportive contexts, from epilepsy inpatients and older adults with HIV to school programs and women after prolapse surgery. (PMIDs 42700754, 42671694, 41533689, 42785145; first seen 2026-09-02)
- **recovery-and-psych-state-predict-injury** — In competitive athletes, poor recovery status and pre-race psychological traits such as stress and sleep quality predict injury better than training load. (PMIDs 42687223, 41388586; first seen 2026-09-03)
- **priming-before-skill-practice** — Acute aerobic exercise improved motor learning only when performed before skill practice, and practitioner frameworks propose similar preconditioning logic for performance enhancement. (PMIDs 42692380, 42136134; first seen 2026-09-02)
- **first-contact-physiotherapy** — Rehabilitation professionals are being placed at the point of first musculoskeletal contact, in general practice and in joint surgeon-therapist consultations, and are rated well prepared for the role. (PMIDs 42644384, 41895244, 42782070; first seen 2026-09-02)
- **protein-dose-above-adequacy** — Once protein and energy intake are adequate, extra protein source, amino acid or carbohydrate loading gives only marginal or outcome-specific recovery and adaptation benefits. (PMIDs 42248424, 42696639, 42700960; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **concussion-burden-and-early-markers** — Adolescent sport-related concussion is linked to lower quality of life a decade later, and clinic exercise intolerance testing doubles the detected odds of prolonged recovery. (PMIDs 42696730, 42703626; first seen 2026-09-09)
- **exercise-safe-in-cautioned-populations** — Vigorous or high-impact activity appears safe or beneficial in groups usually advised to hold back, including hip osteoarthritis patients and women with twin pregnancies. (PMIDs 42414085, 42754383; first seen 2026-09-24)
