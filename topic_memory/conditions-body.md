# Topic Memory: Conditions & Body
_Last updated: 2026-09-28 · source_id: conditions-body · covers studies through 2026-09-27_

## Established findings
- **autism-transition-support-gaps** — Qualitative studies of autistic people and caregivers consistently find inadequate preparation and structural support at care transitions, with First Nations caregivers additionally describing racism and burnout alongside cultural reframing of autism. (PMIDs 42572330, 42596819, 42631645, 42517456)
- **paradoxical-inflammation-on-immune-targeted-drugs** — Case reports describe new inflammatory disease or switched skin phenotypes during immune-targeted therapy, including pustulosis on secukinumab, giant cell arteritis on risankizumab, psoriasis on pembrolizumab, and lymphoma reports on dupilumab. (PMIDs 42565330, 42598823, 42607573, 42675660, 42685682)
- **ai-diagnostic-accuracy-vs-readiness** — Across imaging, pathology, embryology, sperm selection and safety-incident classification, AI performs at roughly expert level but clinicians remain equal or better and prospective validation and governance are missing. (PMIDs 42615559, 42628962, 42758072, 42768793)
- **trial-endpoints-miss-patient-benefit** — Conventional trial endpoints understate patient-relevant benefit and burden: quality of life is rarely primary, symptom remission leaves function impaired, participation demands have risen, and unplanned hospital visits are common. (PMIDs 42447620, 42581066, 42695153, 42696696)
- **methylation-classifiers-for-cancer-detection** — DNA methylation profiling separated disease states in blood-based ovarian cancer detection, a 3-gene anal swab classifier for high-grade lesions, and head and neck squamous subtype classification. (PMIDs 42552441, 42605682, 42660820)
- **food-challenge-safety** — Supervised oral food challenges and graded home ladder protocols in milk, egg and wheat allergy carry low severe-reaction risk, with under 1% to about 2% anaphylaxis and rare epinephrine use. (PMIDs 42663073, 42671940, 42529903, 42698161)
- **polyclonal-resistance-after-osimertinib** — First-line osimertinib in EGFR-mutant lung cancer is followed by near-universal, often multiple resistance alterations, and only about a quarter of patients achieve multi-year progression-free and overall survival. (PMIDs 41790029, 42061572, 42767607, 42790325)

## Emerging threads
- **first-episode-monocyte-inflammation** — Objective immune abnormalities appear at first psychiatric episodes: raised monocyte counts and monocyte-to-lymphocyte ratios in drug-naive psychosis and mania, and cerebrospinal fluid abnormalities in roughly one in fourteen first-episode psychosis patients. (PMIDs 42112712, 42692293, 42764133; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **blood-inflammation-weak-in-depression** — Routine blood inflammatory measures relate poorly to depressive symptom severity, with complete-blood-count ratios failing to predict postpartum depression and childhood maltreatment outperforming hs-CRP in first-episode depression. (PMIDs 42627764, 42673683; first seen 2026-08-28)
- **postpartum-burden-underrecognized** — Postpartum morbidity is underrecognized: 13% of women still had perineal pain at 90 days after vaginal birth, mothers reported more burnout than fathers, and a partner's ADHD raised maternal anxiety. (PMIDs 42686059, 39773292, 41982066; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **maternal-perinatal-mood-infant-development** — Maternal depressive symptoms that persist across the perinatal period, rather than isolated episodes, predict poorer infant neurodevelopment in preterm babies and more difficult temperament at two years. (PMIDs 42778033, 42779328; first seen 2026-09-26)
- **women-report-greater-skin-disease-burden** — Women and gender-diverse patients report greater burden across conditions: worse quality of life in mild hidradenitis, less expected treatment benefit, higher discrimination-trauma symptoms, and earlier, female-concentrated adolescent self-harm. (PMIDs 42627116, 42658205, 42765968, 42785388; first seen 2026-08-26)
