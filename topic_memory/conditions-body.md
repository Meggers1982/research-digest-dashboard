# Topic Memory: Conditions & Body
_Last updated: 2026-09-14 · source_id: conditions-body · covers studies through 2026-09-13_

## Established findings
- **autism-transition-support-gaps** — Qualitative studies of autistic people and their caregivers consistently find inadequate preparation and structural support at care transitions, with First Nations caregivers additionally describing racism and burnout alongside cultural reframing of autism. (PMIDs 42572330, 42596819, 42631645, 42517456)
- **paradoxical-inflammation-on-immune-targeted-drugs** — Case reports describe new inflammatory disease or switched skin phenotypes during immune-targeted therapy, including pustulosis on secukinumab, giant cell arteritis on risankizumab, psoriasis on pembrolizumab, and cutaneous T-cell lymphoma reports on dupilumab. (PMIDs 42565330, 42598823, 42607573, 42675660, 42685682)
- **ai-diagnostic-accuracy-vs-readiness** — Across imaging, pathology, embryology and record-based prognosis, AI tools report high accuracy, but reviews and reader comparisons find clinicians equal or better and prospective validation and governance missing. (PMIDs 42615559, 42595811, 42628962, 42698363, 42696697)
- **phase1-tolerable-few-responses** — First-in-human oncology trials of a p53-reactivating peptide, an anti-CD96 antibody, and a CBP/beta-catenin inhibitor each showed acceptable safety and target engagement but almost no objective tumor responses. (PMIDs 42189874, 42658188, 42243349)
- **trial-endpoints-miss-patient-benefit** — Conventional endpoints understate patient-relevant benefit and burden: quality of life is rarely primary, symptom remission leaves function impaired, trial time demands have risen, and unplanned hospital visits during chemotherapy are common. (PMIDs 42447620, 42581066, 42695153, 42696696)
- **methylation-classifiers-for-cancer-detection** — DNA methylation profiling separated disease states in blood-based ovarian cancer detection, a 3-gene anal swab classifier for high-grade lesions, and head and neck squamous subtype classification. (PMIDs 42552441, 42605682, 42660820)
- **food-challenge-safety** — Supervised oral food challenges and graded home ladder protocols in milk, egg and wheat allergy carry low severe-reaction risk, with under 1% to about 2% anaphylaxis and rare epinephrine use. (PMIDs 42663073, 42671940, 42529903, 42698161)

## Emerging threads
- **polyclonal-resistance-after-osimertinib** — Tissue and plasma sequencing after first-line osimertinib in EGFR-mutant lung cancer found resistance alterations in about 87% of patients and multiple simultaneous mechanisms in 46%, with amivantamab plus lazertinib shifting that landscape. (PMIDs 41790029, 42430304, 42061572; first seen 2026-09-06)
- **real-world-olaparib-ovarian** — Retrospective Asian cohorts of olaparib maintenance in ovarian cancer reproduced trial-level progression-free survival in both first relapse and first-line BRCA-mutated disease, with frequent grade 3 anemia. (PMIDs 42565791, 42642151; first seen 2026-08-10)
- **off-label-drugs-vulvar-lichen-sclerosus** — Small uncontrolled series report symptom relief in therapy-resistant vulvar lichen sclerosus with topical ruxolitinib and with low-dose methotrexate in cases complicated by HPV-independent vulvar intraepithelial neoplasia. (PMIDs 42587402, 42663040; first seen 2026-08-16)
- **blood-inflammation-weak-in-depression** — Routine blood inflammatory measures relate poorly to depressive symptom severity: complete-blood-count ratios did not predict postpartum depression, and childhood maltreatment outperformed hs-CRP in first-episode, drug-naive major depression. (PMIDs 42627764, 42673683; first seen 2026-08-28)
- **first-episode-monocyte-inflammation** — In drug-naive first-episode psychosis and first-episode mania, monocyte counts and monocyte-to-lymphocyte ratios were elevated versus matched controls, though both reports come from single Turkish centers. (PMIDs 42112712, 42692293; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **postpartum-burden-underrecognized** — Postpartum morbidity is underrecognized: 13% of women still had perineal pain at 90 days after vaginal birth, mothers reported more burnout than fathers, and a partner's ADHD raised maternal anxiety. (PMIDs 42686059, 39773292, 41982066; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **women-report-greater-skin-disease-burden** — In dermatology cohorts, women with even mild hidradenitis suppurativa reported worse quality of life than men, and women in routine clinics expected and perceived less treatment benefit. (PMIDs 42627116, 42658205; first seen 2026-08-26)
