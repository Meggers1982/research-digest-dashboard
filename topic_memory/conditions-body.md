# Topic Memory: Conditions & Body
_Last updated: 2026-11-09 · source_id: conditions-body · covers studies through 2026-11-08_

## Established findings
- **autism-transition-support-gaps** — Autistic people and caregivers consistently describe inadequate preparation and structural support at care transitions, with First Nations caregivers additionally reporting racism and burnout alongside cultural reframing of autism. (PMIDs 42572330, 42596819, 42631645, 42517456, 42770208)
- **paradoxical-inflammation-on-immune-targeted-drugs** — Case reports describe new inflammatory disease or switched skin phenotypes during immune-targeted therapy, including pustulosis on secukinumab, giant cell arteritis on risankizumab, psoriasis on pembrolizumab, and lymphoma on dupilumab. (PMIDs 42565330, 42598823, 42607573, 42675660, 42685682)
- **ai-diagnostic-accuracy-vs-readiness** — Clinical AI performs near expert level in narrow tasks, but prospective validation, governance and data sharing are missing, and unrepresentative or siloed data threaten accuracy for underserved patients. (PMIDs 42758072, 42768793, 42808945, 42852990, 42960413)
- **trial-endpoints-miss-patient-benefit** — Conventional endpoints and clinician ratings understate patient-relevant benefit and burden: quality of life is rarely primary, symptom remission leaves function impaired, early physical decline goes undetected, and social harm is unrecorded. (PMIDs 42447620, 42695153, 42696696, 42867277, 42965695)
- **polyclonal-resistance-after-osimertinib** — First-line osimertinib in EGFR-mutant lung cancer is followed by frequent early progression and near-universal resistance alterations, with high PD-L1 predicting early failure and salvage chemotherapy combinations helping only a minority. (PMIDs 42767607, 42790325, 42913244)
- **first-episode-monocyte-inflammation** — Drug-naive patients at a first psychotic or manic episode show objective peripheral abnormalities: raised monocyte and composite blood inflammatory indices, cytokine and cerebrospinal fluid shifts, and distinguishing serum lipid metabolite profiles. (PMIDs 42112712, 42692293, 42305038, 42925955)
- **postpartum-burden-underrecognized** — Postpartum morbidity is underrecognized and poorly remedied: perineal pain persists at 90 days, maternal burnout exceeds paternal, and position changes or platelet-rich plasma lack proven benefit for perineal and cesarean wounds. (PMIDs 42686059, 39773292, 41982066, 42771819, 42807524)
- **rare-variant-infertility-genetics** — Whole-exome sequencing of small cohorts or single families keeps identifying novel single-gene causes of infertility, including ODF2, ADGRG2 and ZPBP in men and maternal GMNN in embryonic arrest. (PMIDs 42886617, 42895690, 42920325)

## Emerging threads
- **first-trimester-preeclampsia-screening-calibration** — First-trimester preeclampsia screening algorithms misclassify risk across conception modes and ancestries, with programmed frozen embryo transfer altering Doppler markers and European models needing recalibration elsewhere. (PMIDs 42950944, 42452901, 42383395; first seen 2026-11-04)
- **genomic-profiling-implementation-gap** — Genomic profiling and molecular tumor boards generate actionable recommendations and survival gains, but implementation, cross-country access and non-European ancestry evidence lag far behind the testing itself. (PMIDs 42759265, 42141746, 42854350, 42958622; first seen 2026-11-08)
- **caregiver-practical-support-gaps** — Family caregivers across childhood cancer, survivorship, rare syndromes and aging-parent care report financial strain, exhaustion and no practical guidance from clinicians. (PMIDs 40249918, 42955377, 42287271; first seen 2026-11-05)
- **psychedelics-safety-early-trials** — Small early-phase studies of MDMA- and psilocybin-assisted therapy report symptom reduction and no cognitive harm, but samples are tiny and uncontrolled. (PMIDs 42962981, 42490582; first seen 2026-11-06)
- **cosmetic-ingredient-lab-only-evidence** — Skin ingredient research, often industry-funded, reports anti-inflammatory, barrier and depigmenting benefits only in cells, explants or literature reviews, with clinical human trial data still absent. (PMIDs 42937004, 42960918, 42984850; first seen 2026-10-29)
- **women-report-greater-skin-disease-burden** — Women and gender-diverse patients report greater burden across conditions: worse quality of life in mild hidradenitis, less expected treatment benefit, higher discrimination-trauma symptoms, and earlier, female-concentrated adolescent self-harm. (PMIDs 42627116, 42658205, 42765968, 42785388; first seen 2026-08-26)
