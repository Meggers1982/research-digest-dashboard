# Topic Memory: Conditions & Body
_Last updated: 2026-10-05 · source_id: conditions-body · covers studies through 2026-10-04_

## Established findings
- **autism-transition-support-gaps** — Autistic people and caregivers consistently describe inadequate preparation and structural support at care transitions, with First Nations caregivers additionally reporting racism and burnout alongside cultural reframing of autism. (PMIDs 42572330, 42596819, 42631645, 42517456, 42770208)
- **paradoxical-inflammation-on-immune-targeted-drugs** — Case reports describe new inflammatory disease or switched skin phenotypes during immune-targeted therapy, including pustulosis on secukinumab, giant cell arteritis on risankizumab, psoriasis on pembrolizumab, and lymphoma on dupilumab. (PMIDs 42565330, 42598823, 42607573, 42675660, 42685682)
- **ai-diagnostic-accuracy-vs-readiness** — Clinical AI performs near expert level in narrow tasks, but prospective validation and governance are missing and unrepresentative training data threaten accuracy for darker skin and rare pediatric disease. (PMIDs 42628962, 42758072, 42768793, 42808945, 42600230)
- **trial-endpoints-miss-patient-benefit** — Conventional trial endpoints understate patient-relevant benefit and burden: quality of life is rarely primary, symptom remission leaves function impaired, participation demands have risen, and unplanned hospital visits are common. (PMIDs 42447620, 42581066, 42695153, 42696696)
- **food-challenge-safety** — Supervised oral food challenges and graded home ladder protocols in milk, egg and wheat allergy carry low severe-reaction risk, with under 1% to about 2% anaphylaxis and rare epinephrine use. (PMIDs 42663073, 42671940, 42529903, 42698161)
- **polyclonal-resistance-after-osimertinib** — First-line osimertinib in EGFR-mutant lung cancer is followed by near-universal, often multiple resistance alterations, and only about a quarter of patients achieve multi-year progression-free and overall survival. (PMIDs 41790029, 42061572, 42767607, 42790325)
- **first-episode-monocyte-inflammation** — Drug-naive patients at a first psychotic or manic episode show objective immune abnormalities: raised monocyte counts and ratios, cerebrospinal fluid changes, and cytokine shifts tied to prolactin and symptom severity. (PMIDs 42112712, 42692293, 42764133, 42800290)
- **postpartum-burden-underrecognized** — Postpartum morbidity is underrecognized and poorly remedied: perineal pain persists at 90 days, maternal burnout exceeds paternal, and position changes or platelet-rich plasma lack proven benefit for perineal and cesarean wounds. (PMIDs 42686059, 39773292, 41982066, 42771819, 42807524)
- **maternal-perinatal-mood-infant-development** — Maternal depressive symptoms persisting across the perinatal period, and maternal mental health hospitalization, predict poorer infant neurodevelopment, more difficult temperament, and more avoidable child hospitalizations. (PMIDs 42778033, 42779328, 42811250)

## Emerging threads
- **blood-inflammation-weak-in-depression** — Routine blood inflammatory measures relate poorly to depressive symptom severity, with complete-blood-count ratios failing to predict postpartum depression and childhood maltreatment outperforming hs-CRP in first-episode depression. (PMIDs 42627764, 42673683; first seen 2026-08-28)
- **women-report-greater-skin-disease-burden** — Women and gender-diverse patients report greater burden across conditions: worse quality of life in mild hidradenitis, less expected treatment benefit, higher discrimination-trauma symptoms, and earlier, female-concentrated adolescent self-harm. (PMIDs 42627116, 42658205, 42765968, 42785388; first seen 2026-08-26)
- **art-lab-innovations-lack-clinical-outcomes** — Assisted reproduction laboratory innovations — three-step sperm recovery, microfluidic sperm selection and matrix-free ovarian organoids — report technical gains without pregnancy or live-birth outcomes. (PMIDs 41474209, 41718015, 42229608; first seen 2026-10-04)
- **gynecologic-diagnosis-delays** — Women with gynecologic conditions reach specialist care late or mismanaged: enhanced transvaginal ultrasound missed most deep endometriosis, and a quarter of obstructed hemivagina patients had prior incomplete procedures. (PMIDs 42808571, 42097447; first seen 2026-10-04)
