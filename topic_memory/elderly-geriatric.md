# Topic Memory: Elderly & Geriatric

## Emerging threads
- **ai-classification-routine-data** — Machine learning on routinely collected data classifies or predicts disease status, procedural success, and injury risk accurately, but reviews find most models unvalidated externally and poorly explainable. (PMIDs 42663162, 42497610, 42671941, 42362108, 41335345, 42678181; first seen 2026-09-01)
- **goals-of-care-conversations-late** — Goals-of-care and prognosis conversations happen late or briefly, and even documented preferences translate poorly into care, with DNR status linked to less regional analgesia and refusals of ventilation resisted. (PMIDs 42250861, 42337874, 42697603, 42690839; first seen 2026-09-01)
- **cachexia-malnutrition-underaddressed** — Cancer cachexia and malnutrition carry eating-related distress and identity harm that clinicians rarely discuss, and the field still lacks aligned definitions and endpoints to guide treatment. (PMIDs 42203496, 42417089, 42405519, 42544933; first seen 2026-09-04)
- **simple-geriatric-screens-predict-outcomes** — Low-burden measures — a claims-based frailty index, ICOPE intrinsic-capacity screening, and post-operative apathy — predict hip fracture hospitalization, sensory and mood deficits, and poor functional recovery. (PMIDs 42675319, 41379576, 42639683; first seen 2026-09-04)
- **acp-uptake-interventions** — Education, EHR alerts, nurse-led conversations, and community health worker programs raise advance care planning documentation or confidence, but income and family conflict still limit completion and none measure goal concordance. (PMIDs 42082327, 42343081, 42681826, 42699999, 42198911; first seen 2026-09-03)
- **palliative-access-barriers** — Palliative care access is limited by stigma in opioid use disorder, almost no tested models for culturally and linguistically diverse patients, and teams unprepared for conditions such as interstitial lung disease. (PMIDs 42141843, 42658237, 42044702, 42695220; first seen 2026-09-02)
- **dementia-shifting-hospice-population** — Dementia is replacing cancer as a major hospice population with longer lengths of stay, while family caregivers report palliative care arriving too late and ending too soon. (PMIDs 42136049, 41247831; first seen 2026-09-06)
- **ai-in-palliative-care** — Large language models perform well on palliative care knowledge tasks and are proposed for ambient communication coaching, but the evidence base remains tiny with unresolved trust and ethics gaps. (PMIDs 42231120, 40598879, 42697288; first seen 2026-09-05)
- **uk-biobank-oa-risk-factors** — UK Biobank analyses link short sleep duration and accelerated biological aging to higher osteoarthritis incidence, with the aging association strongest after age 60. (PMIDs 41568519, 42687813; first seen 2026-09-06)
- **lewy-body-heterogeneity** — Lewy body disease shows marked clinical heterogeneity, including a brain-first versus body-first origin model, an SNCA family with onset from 28 to 51, and biomarker-positive late-onset psychosis. (PMIDs 42463844, 42660007, 42575875; first seen 2026-09-01)
- **screening-monitoring-narrow-care-gaps** — Social-need screening with social work referral, remote electronic symptom monitoring, and virtual pain coping skills training each narrowed socioeconomic or racial gaps in health, pain, or quality of life at acceptable cost. (PMIDs 41556546, 42242438, 42713766; first seen 2026-09-03)
- **family-caregiver-needs-palliative** — Family caregivers in palliative care describe their own needs as invisible in clinical conversations; reviews and an education trial show knowledge plus emotional support reduces distress but is rarely delivered together. (PMIDs 42685648, 42693513, 40938148; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **imaging-symptom-discordance-rheumatic** — In rheumatic disease, imaging detects structural and vascular damage that symptoms understate, including asymptomatic radiographic osteoarthritis in older rheumatoid arthritis patients and subclinical carotid plaque in systemic sclerosis. (PMIDs 42709298, 42605053, 42703781; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **virtual-remote-delivery-older-adults** — Virtually delivered dance, palliative telehealth, and pain coping programs for older and seriously ill adults proved implementable across settings, trusted by patients, and cost-effective. (PMIDs 41166741, 42099245, 42713766; first seen 2026-09-07)
