# Topic Memory: Elderly & Geriatric
_Last updated: 2026-11-09 · source_id: elderly-geriatric · covers studies through 2026-11-08_

## Established findings
- **virtual-remote-delivery-older-adults** — Virtually delivered palliative, rehabilitation and lifestyle programs match or beat usual care, sustain engagement, and gain in patient-stated value once older adults have tried them. (PMIDs 41166741, 42713766, 42749466, 42782261, 42610950, 42966905)
- **goals-of-care-conversations-late** — Documented end-of-life preferences remain rare, are triggered mainly by hospitalization, arrive close to death, and translate poorly into the care patients and families expect. (PMIDs 42250861, 42337874, 42790554, 42766491, 42781834, 42165567)
- **simple-geriatric-screens-predict-outcomes** — Low-burden measures — claims-based frailty indices, grip strength, balance, step counts, motor and cognitive screens, and routine blood indices — predict mortality, falls and functional decline. (PMIDs 41379576, 42639683, 42806885, 42888018, 42900878, 42960478, 42950710)
- **palliative-access-barriers** — Specialty palliative care reaches rural, minority, immigrant, disabled and low-income patients least, and services rarely accommodate the language, religious practice or cultural framing these patients rely on. (PMIDs 42141843, 42658237, 42773632, 42386162, 42860763, 42929285, 42895139)
- **family-caregiver-needs-palliative** — Informal caregivers absorb coordination, medication and bedside symptom work without training, and report depression, anxiety, isolation, financial strain and unmet needs of their own. (PMIDs 42685648, 42693513, 42771517, 42888399, 40899339, 41512003)
- **dignity-relational-end-of-life** — End-of-life dignity and treatment choices hinge on relational factors — clinician word choice, listening and cultural understanding — which burnout and language mismatch erode. (PMIDs 42861474, 42842360, 42910992)
- **ai-in-palliative-care** — Large language models and conversational AI approach clinician accuracy on palliative knowledge, geriatric vulnerability screening and qualitative analysis, but validation is thin and patients trust AI-assisted clinicians less. (PMIDs 42231120, 40598879, 42697288, 42599269, 42950609, 42965520)
- **acp-uptake-interventions** — Simple prompts and structured pathways — paper tools, EHR nudges, nurse conversations, a mandatory prognostic question — increase advance care planning, hospice referral and earlier palliative involvement. (PMIDs 42681826, 42778351, 42806888, 42431717, 42580497)
- **rheumatic-remission-de-escalation** — Remission improves long-term rheumatic outcomes but is often short-lived, with relapse and biologic failure frequent and predictable from residual activity and baseline features. (PMIDs 42767416, 42765471, 42778334, 42839717, 42988546, 42772989, 42977214)

## Emerging threads
- **noncancer-palliative-gap** — Patients dying of dementia and neurological disease enter palliative and hospice care later and sicker than cancer patients, under benefit structures designed for cancer trajectories. (PMIDs 40831402, 42977228; first seen 2026-11-08)
- **frontline-dementia-care-staff-underprepared** — Nursing home and home care workers find dementia with co-occurring mental illness, refusal and aggression the hardest care to give, and feel untrained and unsafe. (PMIDs 41172263, 41316828, 42967328; first seen 2026-11-03)
- **existential-distress-end-of-life** — Spiritual concerns, loneliness, demoralization and unexplained dreams or visions are common near death and distress families, while clinicians report little preparation to respond. (PMIDs 42415544, 42492599, 42343105, 42918739, 42896718; first seen 2026-10-26)
- **rehab-outcome-measures-miss-function** — Standard rehabilitation assessments capture impairment rather than everyday function, so instrumented and participation-focused measures reveal deficits and fatigue that routine scales miss. (PMIDs 42788421, 40519080, 42838842, 42924528, 42956919, 42448259; first seen 2026-09-29)
- **low-value-care-older-adults** — Older adults frequently receive guideline-discordant care, from imaging instead of physical therapy for new back pain to near-universal hospice deprescribing without symptom-outcome evidence. (PMIDs 42852584, 42848201, 42897830; first seen 2026-10-15)
- **eol-delirium-recognition-gap** — Delirium in dying and hospitalized older patients is often unnamed by staff, left to families at home, and the sedatives used show no measurable calming effect. (PMIDs 42902770, 42910992, 42924868; first seen 2026-10-26)
