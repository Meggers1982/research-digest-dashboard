# Topic Memory: Elderly & Geriatric
_Last updated: 2026-10-26 · source_id: elderly-geriatric · covers studies through 2026-10-25_

## Established findings
- **virtual-remote-delivery-older-adults** — Virtually delivered palliative, rehabilitation and creative-arts programs for older and seriously ill adults are implementable, cost-effective and cut cancellations, though rural uptake remains low. (PMIDs 41166741, 42713766, 42749466, 42782261, 42772608)
- **goals-of-care-conversations-late** — Documented end-of-life preferences remain rare, are triggered mainly by hospitalization, arrive close to death, and translate poorly into the care patients and families expect. (PMIDs 42250861, 42337874, 42790554, 42766491, 42781834, 42165567)
- **simple-geriatric-screens-predict-outcomes** — Low-burden measures — claims-based frailty indices, grip strength and its weekly decline, perturbation balance tests and wearable step counts — predict mortality, surgical risk and functional decline. (PMIDs 41379576, 42639683, 42806885, 42867452, 42888018, 42897509, 42901866)
- **palliative-access-barriers** — Specialty palliative care reaches rural, minority, pediatric, disabled and low-income patients least, and even formally flagged unmet need rarely produces a consult before a crisis hospitalization. (PMIDs 42141843, 42658237, 42773632, 42785972, 42386162, 42860763, 42863588, 42860167)
- **family-caregiver-needs-palliative** — Family caregivers in palliative care absorb invisible coordination and information-relay work, face structural barriers to home end-of-life support, and report moderate depression and anxiety. (PMIDs 42685648, 42693513, 42771517, 42888399)
- **dignity-relational-end-of-life** — End-of-life dignity and treatment choices hinge on relational factors — clinician word choice, listening and cultural understanding — which burnout and language mismatch erode. (PMIDs 42861474, 42842360, 42910992)

## Emerging threads
- **rehab-outcome-measures-miss-function** — Rehabilitation programs and assessments capture impairment, strength or engagement rather than everyday function, so measured gains do not translate into quality of life or independence. (PMIDs 42788421, 40519080, 42838842; first seen 2026-09-29)
- **ai-in-palliative-care** — Large language models perform well on palliative knowledge and empathic communication tasks and older adults accept AI diagnostics, but validation is thin and ethical safeguards unsettled. (PMIDs 42231120, 40598879, 42697288, 42599269, 42803724; first seen 2026-09-05)
- **acp-uptake-interventions** — Simple prompts — paper tools, EHR nudges, nurse-led conversations and a yes/no admission screening question — increase advance care planning documentation and bring palliative consultation earlier. (PMIDs 42681826, 42778351, 42806888; first seen 2026-09-03)
- **rheumatic-remission-de-escalation** — Remission now arrives faster but is less durable, with relapse rising over three decades, residual low disease activity predicting flare and prolonged glucocorticoids adding harm without benefit. (PMIDs 42767416, 42765471, 42778334, 42839717, 42919621; first seen 2026-09-23)
- **clinician-bereavement-burnout** — Cumulative patient-death exposure with little bereavement training or organizational support predicts compassion fatigue, burnout and intent to leave among nurses, oncology trainees and palliative volunteers. (PMIDs 42764782, 42778530, 42285425; first seen 2026-09-28)
- **low-value-care-older-adults** — Older adults frequently receive guideline-discordant first-line care, such as imaging instead of physical therapy for new low back pain, and insurance expansion can raise hospitalization. (PMIDs 42852584, 42848201; first seen 2026-10-15)
