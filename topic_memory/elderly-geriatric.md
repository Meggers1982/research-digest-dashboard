# Topic Memory: Elderly & Geriatric
_Last updated: 2026-09-28 · source_id: elderly-geriatric · covers studies through 2026-09-27_

## Established findings
- **virtual-remote-delivery-older-adults** — Virtually delivered palliative, rehabilitation and creative-arts programs for older and seriously ill adults are implementable, cost-effective and cut cancellations, though rural uptake remains low. (PMIDs 41166741, 42713766, 42749466, 42782261, 42772608)
- **goals-of-care-conversations-late** — Prognosis, device deactivation and palliative-need conversations occur close to death or after needs have long emerged, and documented preferences translate poorly into care. (PMIDs 42250861, 42337874, 42790554, 42766491)
- **simple-geriatric-screens-predict-outcomes** — Low-burden measures — claims-based frailty indices, intrinsic-capacity screening, handgrip and inspiratory muscle strength — predict mortality, frailty and functional decline in older adults. (PMIDs 41379576, 42639683, 42760446, 42765717)

## Emerging threads
- **palliative-access-barriers** — Specialty palliative care reaches Black, Indigenous, rural and low- or middle-income-country patients least, limited by workforce and training shortages, opioid supply and referral inequity rather than demand. (PMIDs 42141843, 42658237, 42773632, 42751732, 42785972, 42764785; first seen 2026-09-02)
- **acp-uptake-interventions** — Education, EHR prompts, nurse-led conversations and decision aids raise advance care planning documentation, with simple paper-based tools ranking ahead of electronic and video formats. (PMIDs 42681826, 42778351; first seen 2026-09-03)
- **ai-classification-routine-data** — Prediction and classification models built on routine, imaging or public genomic data report moderate to good accuracy in derivation samples but are rarely externally validated. (PMIDs 42663162, 42497610, 42751923, 42768911; first seen 2026-09-01)
- **rheumatic-remission-de-escalation** — In rheumatic disease in sustained remission, continued glucocorticoids add no benefit and biologic withdrawal is often durable, with biomarkers beginning to identify flare risk. (PMIDs 42767416, 42765471, 42778334; first seen 2026-09-23)
- **cachexia-malnutrition-underaddressed** — Cancer cachexia and malnutrition carry eating-related distress and identity harm clinicians rarely discuss, and the field lacks aligned definitions and endpoints. (PMIDs 42203496, 42417089, 42405519, 42544933; first seen 2026-09-04)
- **family-caregiver-needs-palliative** — Family caregivers in palliative care describe their needs as invisible, including anticipatory grief during hospital-to-home transitions, and rarely receive knowledge and emotional support together. (PMIDs 42685648, 42693513, 40938148, 42771517; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **dementia-shifting-hospice-population** — Dementia is replacing cancer as a major hospice population with longer lengths of stay, while families report palliative care arriving too late and ending too soon. (PMIDs 42136049, 41247831; first seen 2026-09-06)
- **ai-in-palliative-care** — Large language models perform well on palliative care knowledge tasks and are proposed for communication coaching, but the evidence base is tiny with unresolved trust and ethics gaps. (PMIDs 42231120, 40598879, 42697288; first seen 2026-09-05)
- **uk-biobank-oa-risk-factors** — UK Biobank analyses link short sleep, accelerated biological aging and multimorbidity to incident osteoarthritis and sarcopenia, with associations strongest after age 60. (PMIDs 41568519, 42687813, 42764080; first seen 2026-09-06)
- **lewy-body-heterogeneity** — Lewy body disease shows marked clinical heterogeneity, including brain-first versus body-first origins, early-onset SNCA families, and biomarker-positive late-onset psychosis. (PMIDs 42463844, 42660007, 42575875; first seen 2026-09-01)
- **imaging-symptom-discordance-rheumatic** — In rheumatic disease, imaging detects structural and vascular damage that symptoms understate, including asymptomatic radiographic osteoarthritis and subclinical carotid plaque. (PMIDs 42709298, 42605053, 42703781; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **psychosocial-meaning-late-life** — Subjective disengagement — feeling out of step with the times, boredom and low purpose — predicts later loneliness and weaker episodic memory in older adults. (PMIDs 42754696, 42773782; first seen 2026-09-24)
