# Topic Memory: Cardiology & Heart Health
_Last updated: 2026-10-23 · source_id: cardiology-heart · covers studies through 2026-10-18_

## Established findings
- **new-ablation-hardware-single-arm** — New interventional cardiac and vascular hardware and techniques are reported almost exclusively in small single-arm, single-center, often industry-funded series with surrogate endpoints. (PMIDs 42648460, 42687085, 42859373, 42840917, 42863882, 42857978)
- **rare-blood-group-variants-transfusion** — Rare blood group and hemoglobin variants, engineered therapeutic antibodies and competing classification rules repeatedly confound standard hematology and hemostasis test interpretation. (PMIDs 42678021, 42624819, 42687636, 42671022, 42775622, 42788691)
- **type2-biologics-airway-disease** — Biologics for type 2 airway disease give comparable benefit across agents and reduce steroid burden, with earlier initiation showing strong exacerbation reductions. (PMIDs 42546934, 42716466, 42778034)
- **blood-markers-copd-exacerbation-risk** — Routine blood-derived markers stratify exacerbation frequency and in-hospital outcomes in hospitalized COPD patients. (PMIDs 42631219, 42611378, 42337860)
- **noninvasive-ph-assessment** — Bedside ultrasound, ECG T-wave inversion burden, cardiac MRI coupling surrogates and serum markers detect high pulmonary vascular resistance and right ventricular dysfunction without catheterization. (PMIDs 42668061, 42648467, 42857453, 42849796)

## Emerging threads
- **cv-risk-prediction-models-marginal-gain** — Biomarker, ECG, imaging and AI tools in cardiopulmonary care report discrimination against surrogate endpoints, usually single-center and without external validation or evidence that use changes outcomes. (PMIDs 42857453, 42849796, 42869272, 42876388, 42858948; first seen 2026-10-14)
- **underuse-indicated-cv-care** — Indicated cardiovascular diagnostics and therapies are underused, with access shaped by race, age assumptions, country income, workforce training, sex and local catheterization capacity. (PMIDs 42842171, 42833700, 42869176, 42844063, 42865129; first seen 2026-10-12)
- **breath-analysis-surrogate-only** — Exhaled breath profiling distinguishes pneumonia, sarcoidosis and glucose disturbance in small internally validated samples, but platforms remain proof-of-concept with no clinical decision evidence. (PMIDs 42771588, 42805222, 42727601, 42777743; first seen 2026-09-28)
- **aortic-valve-planning-imaging-surrogate** — Lifetime valve planning frameworks, 4D flow MRI, revised stress echo criteria and extended reality are advanced for aortic valve care on surrogate or conceptual grounds only. (PMIDs 42648856, 42770219, 40780565, 42833700; first seen 2026-09-29)
- **platelet-thromboinflammation-copd** — Platelet activation markers predict COPD exacerbations and death in smokers, and cardiovascular risk concentrates in the weeks after a severe exacerbation. (PMIDs 42690694, 42696427, 42800650; first seen 2026-09-10)
- **early-phase-pah-therapeutics** — Emerging pulmonary vascular therapies, including denervation and pulmonary arterial hypertension drugs, rest on small, open-label or nonrandomized studies with surrogate endpoints. (PMIDs 42728065, 42463617, 42276742, 42840917; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **central-aortic-bp-phenotypes** — Ambulatory central aortic pressure and cuff-derived arterial stiffness indices identify cardiovascular risk phenotypes that standard brachial readings miss, so far against surrogate endpoints only. (PMIDs 42299866, 42307071; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **lung-cancer-screening-gaps-tobacco-cvd** — Lung cancer screening uptake is low among newly eligible long-quit smokers, and benign biopsy results from screen-detected lesions carry residual malignancy risk. (PMIDs 42759647, 42790396, 42832469; first seen 2026-09-24)
- **body-shape-and-adherence-beyond-standard-metrics** — Central adiposity outperforms body mass index for mortality prediction in reduced ejection fraction, and genetic as well as in-hospital factors are proposed drivers of medication adherence. (PMIDs 42340801, 42855850, 42862778; first seen 2026-10-15)
