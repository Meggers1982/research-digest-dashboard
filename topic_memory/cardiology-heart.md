# Topic Memory: Cardiology & Heart Health
_Last updated: 2026-10-05 · source_id: cardiology-heart · covers studies through 2026-10-04_

## Established findings
- **cv-risk-prediction-models-marginal-gain** — Biomarker, imaging, breath-based and AI tools in cardiopulmonary care report discrimination against surrogate endpoints, usually single-center and without external validation or evidence that use changes outcomes. (PMIDs 42622203, 42648391, 42674218, 42751923, 42776134, 42636991, 42521527, 42776068)
- **underuse-indicated-cv-care** — Indicated cardiovascular diagnostics and therapies are underused, with access shaped by country income, workforce training, rural scope-of-practice law, race, sex and pediatric drug availability. (PMIDs 42618427, 42680576, 42727843, 42786661, 42689328, 42826811, 42809138)
- **new-ablation-hardware-single-arm** — New interventional cardiac, vascular and thoracic hardware and techniques are reported almost exclusively in small single-arm, single-center, often industry-funded series with surrogate endpoints. (PMIDs 42648460, 42663338, 42687085, 42702207, 42331280)
- **rare-blood-group-variants-transfusion** — Rare blood group and hemoglobin variants, engineered therapeutic antibodies and competing classification rules repeatedly confound standard hematology and hemostasis test interpretation. (PMIDs 42678021, 42624819, 42687636, 42671022, 42775622, 42788691)
- **type2-biologics-airway-disease** — Biologics for type 2 airway disease give comparable benefit across agents and reduce steroid burden, with earlier initiation and the aspirin-exacerbated phenotype showing strong exacerbation reductions. (PMIDs 42546934, 42716466, 42778034)
- **blood-markers-copd-exacerbation-risk** — Routine blood-derived markers, including irisin with immune-inflammation index, iNOS/eNOS ratio and neutrophil-dominant clusters, stratify exacerbation frequency and in-hospital outcomes in hospitalized COPD patients. (PMIDs 42631219, 42611378, 42337860)

## Emerging threads
- **breath-analysis-surrogate-only** — Exhaled breath profiling distinguishes sarcoidosis, pneumonia and glucose disturbance in small internally validated samples, but platforms remain proof-of-concept with no clinical decision evidence. (PMIDs 42771588, 42805222, 42727601, 42777743; first seen 2026-09-28)
- **aortic-valve-planning-imaging-surrogate** — Lifetime valve planning frameworks, 4D flow MRI, revised stress echo criteria and AI or extended reality are advanced for aortic valve care on surrogate or conceptual grounds only. (PMIDs 42648856, 42770219, 40780565; first seen 2026-09-29)
- **platelet-thromboinflammation-copd** — Platelet activation markers predict COPD exacerbations and death in smokers, and cardiovascular risk concentrates in the weeks immediately after a severe exacerbation. (PMIDs 42690694, 42696427, 42800650; first seen 2026-09-10)
- **noninvasive-ph-assessment** — Bedside VExUS ultrasound, pulmonary artery stiffness with right ventricular interplay and serum NOS levels show promise for detecting and prognosticating pulmonary hypertension without catheterization. (PMIDs 42668061, 42648467, 42611378; first seen 2026-08-25)
- **early-phase-pah-therapeutics** — Emerging pulmonary arterial hypertension and progressive fibrosis therapies rest on small, open-label or nonrandomized studies with surrogate endpoints. (PMIDs 42728065, 42463617, 42276742; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **central-aortic-bp-phenotypes** — Ambulatory central aortic pressure and cuff-derived arterial stiffness indices identify cardiovascular risk phenotypes that standard brachial readings miss, so far against surrogate endpoints only. (PMIDs 42299866, 42307071; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **lung-cancer-screening-gaps-tobacco-cvd** — Lung cancer screening uptake is below one in ten among newly eligible long-quit smokers, while tobacco-exposed patients with atherosclerotic disease may warrant earlier screening. (PMIDs 42759647, 42790396; first seen 2026-09-24)
