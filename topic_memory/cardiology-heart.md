# Topic Memory: Cardiology & Heart Health
_Last updated: 2026-10-26 · source_id: cardiology-heart · covers studies through 2026-10-25_

## Established findings
- **new-ablation-hardware-single-arm** — New interventional cardiac and vascular hardware reaches practice through small single-arm, single-center series with surrogate endpoints, with failures surfacing only as isolated case reports. (PMIDs 42902780, 42478835, 42648460, 42687085, 42840917, 42863882)
- **rare-blood-group-variants-transfusion** — Rare blood group and hemoglobin variants, engineered therapeutic antibodies and competing classification rules repeatedly confound standard hematology, serology and hemostasis test interpretation. (PMIDs 42891508, 42678021, 42624819, 42788691)
- **type2-biologics-airway-disease** — Biologics for type 2 airway disease give comparable benefit across agents, reduce steroid burden, and perform at least as well in real-world cohorts as in registration trials. (PMIDs 42242757, 42546934, 42778034)
- **blood-markers-copd-exacerbation-risk** — Routine blood-derived markers stratify exacerbation frequency and in-hospital outcomes in hospitalized COPD patients. (PMIDs 42631219, 42611378, 42337860)
- **noninvasive-ph-assessment** — Bedside ultrasound, ECG T-wave inversion burden, cardiac MRI coupling and 4D-flow shear stress measures, and serum markers detect pulmonary hypertension and right ventricular dysfunction without catheterization. (PMIDs 42898806, 42668061, 42648467, 42857453)

## Emerging threads
- **cv-risk-prediction-models-marginal-gain** — Biomarker, ECG, imaging and AI tools in cardiopulmonary care report discrimination against surrogate endpoints, usually single-center and without external validation or evidence that use changes outcomes. (PMIDs 42857453, 42849796, 42869272, 42876388, 42858948, 42898806; first seen 2026-10-14)
- **underuse-indicated-cv-care** — Indicated cardiovascular diagnostics and therapies are underused, with access shaped by race, age assumptions, country income, workforce training, sex and local procedural capacity. (PMIDs 42842171, 42833700, 42869176, 42844063, 42865129; first seen 2026-10-12)
- **inhaler-delivery-route-copd** — In COPD, delivery route and device fit to patient capability determine inhaled drug delivery and response more than the drug combination itself. (PMIDs 42887614, 42908195; first seen 2026-10-23)
- **aortic-valve-planning-imaging-surrogate** — Lifetime valve planning frameworks, 4D flow MRI, revised stress echo criteria and extended reality are advanced for aortic valve care on surrogate or conceptual grounds only. (PMIDs 42648856, 42770219, 40780565, 42833700; first seen 2026-09-29)
- **platelet-thromboinflammation-copd** — Platelet activation markers predict COPD exacerbations and death in smokers, and cardiovascular risk concentrates in the weeks after a severe exacerbation. (PMIDs 42690694, 42696427, 42800650; first seen 2026-09-10)
- **early-phase-pah-therapeutics** — Emerging pulmonary vascular therapies, including denervation and pulmonary arterial hypertension drugs, rest on small, open-label or nonrandomized studies with surrogate endpoints, while registry case mix shifts toward older, obese, cardiometabolic patients. (PMIDs 42728065, 42463617, 42276742, 42840917, 42908309; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **body-shape-and-adherence-beyond-standard-metrics** — Adiposity measures relate to cardiovascular outcomes against expectation: central adiposity beats body mass index for mortality in reduced ejection fraction, and higher body mass index tracks with lower inpatient venous thromboembolism. (PMIDs 42340801, 42855850, 42862778, 42885756; first seen 2026-10-15)
- **anti-inflammatory-adjuncts-infection** — Adjunctive immunomodulating antibiotics suppress interleukin-6 and related cytokines in hospitalized pneumonia, with mortality benefit so far limited to subgroups in secondary analyses. (PMIDs 42892349; first seen 2026-10-24)
