# Topic Memory: Cardiology & Heart Health
_Last updated: 2026-11-02 · source_id: cardiology-heart · covers studies through 2026-11-01_

## Established findings
- **new-ablation-hardware-single-arm** — New interventional cardiac and vascular hardware reaches practice through small single-arm or case-series reports with surrogate endpoints, with failures surfacing only as isolated case reports. (PMIDs 42902780, 42913094, 42909613, 42961389, 42478835, 42648460, 42687085)
- **rare-blood-group-variants-transfusion** — Rare blood group and hemoglobin variants, engineered therapeutic antibodies and competing classification rules repeatedly confound standard hematology, serology and hemostasis test interpretation. (PMIDs 42891508, 42678021, 42624819, 42788691)
- **type2-biologics-airway-disease** — Biologics for type 2 airway disease give comparable benefit across agents, reduce steroid burden, and perform at least as well in real-world cohorts as in registration trials. (PMIDs 42242757, 42546934, 42778034)
- **blood-markers-copd-exacerbation-risk** — Routine blood-derived markers stratify exacerbation frequency and in-hospital outcomes in hospitalized COPD patients. (PMIDs 42631219, 42611378, 42337860)
- **noninvasive-ph-assessment** — Bedside ultrasound, ECG T-wave inversion burden, cardiac MRI coupling and 4D-flow measures, and serum markers detect pulmonary hypertension and right ventricular dysfunction without catheterization. (PMIDs 42898806, 42668061, 42648467, 42857453)

## Emerging threads
- **cv-risk-prediction-models-marginal-gain** — Biomarker, imaging, ECG and physiologic prediction tools in cardiopulmonary care report discrimination against surrogate endpoints, usually single-center and without external validation or evidence that use changes outcomes. (PMIDs 42944652, 42901132, 42857453, 42869272, 42858948; first seen 2026-10-14)
- **underuse-indicated-cv-care** — Indicated cardiovascular diagnostics and procedures are underused, with access shaped by race, age, sex, country income and local procedural capacity rather than by achievable outcomes. (PMIDs 42894974, 42904940, 42842171, 42869176, 42844063; first seen 2026-10-12)
- **tricuspid-regurgitation-late-systemic-disease** — Severe tricuspid regurgitation is usually first documented when right heart failure is already advanced, and systemic congestion markers such as liver extracellular volume predict death or heart failure hospitalization. (PMIDs 42901428, 42944652; first seen 2026-10-27)
- **inhaler-delivery-route-copd** — In COPD, delivery route and device fit to patient capability determine inhaled drug delivery and response more than the drug combination itself. (PMIDs 42887614, 42908195; first seen 2026-10-23)
- **aortic-valve-planning-imaging-surrogate** — Lifetime valve planning frameworks, 4D flow MRI, revised stress echo criteria and extended reality are advanced for aortic valve care on surrogate or conceptual grounds only. (PMIDs 42648856, 42770219, 40780565, 42833700; first seen 2026-09-29)
- **platelet-thromboinflammation-copd** — Platelet activation markers predict COPD exacerbations and death in smokers, and cardiovascular risk concentrates in the weeks after a severe exacerbation. (PMIDs 42690694, 42696427, 42800650; first seen 2026-09-10)
- **early-phase-pah-therapeutics** — Emerging pulmonary vascular therapies, including sotatercept and denervation, rest on small, open-label or retrospective single-center studies with surrogate endpoints and short follow-up. (PMIDs 42924171, 42728065, 42463617, 42276742, 42908309; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **body-shape-and-adherence-beyond-standard-metrics** — Adiposity measures relate to cardiovascular outcomes against expectation: central adiposity beats body mass index for mortality in reduced ejection fraction, and higher body mass index tracks with lower inpatient venous thromboembolism. (PMIDs 42340801, 42855850, 42862778, 42885756; first seen 2026-10-15)
