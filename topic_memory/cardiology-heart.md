# Topic Memory: Cardiology & Heart Health
_Last updated: 2026-11-09 · source_id: cardiology-heart · covers studies through 2026-11-08_

## Established findings
- **new-ablation-hardware-single-arm** — New interventional cardiac hardware, mapping metrics and hemodynamic indices reach practice through small single-arm, single-center or case-series reports with surrogate endpoints and frequent industry funding. (PMIDs 42902780, 42913094, 42961389, 42234368, 42118507, 42965544, 42933922)
- **blood-markers-copd-exacerbation-risk** — Routine blood-derived markers stratify exacerbation frequency and in-hospital outcomes in hospitalized COPD patients. (PMIDs 42631219, 42611378, 42337860)
- **noninvasive-ph-assessment** — Bedside ultrasound, ECG, cardiac MRI coupling and 4D-flow measures and serum markers detect pulmonary hypertension and right ventricular dysfunction without catheterization. (PMIDs 42898806, 42668061, 42648467, 42857453)

## Emerging threads
- **underuse-indicated-cv-care** — Indicated cardiovascular and respiratory diagnostics, drugs and procedures are underused, with access shaped by sex, race, rurality, country income and local capacity rather than expected benefit. (PMIDs 42894974, 42904940, 42842171, 42985347, 42833476; first seen 2026-10-12)
- **cv-risk-prediction-models-marginal-gain** — Biomarker, imaging, ECG and physiologic prediction tools in cardiopulmonary care report discrimination against surrogate endpoints, usually single-center and without external validation or evidence that use changes outcomes. (PMIDs 42944652, 42901132, 42869272, 42858948, 42998781, 42855086; first seen 2026-10-14)
- **cardio-oncology-cv-risk-beyond-ldl** — Cancer patients sustain atherosclerotic and cardiotoxic events that standard lipid-based risk assessment misses, with genetic susceptibility beginning to explain who is harmed. (PMIDs 42628824, 42934841; first seen 2026-11-03)
- **subclinical-markers-before-copd** — Spirometric phenotypes and CT-derived pulmonary arterial volume identify smokers at risk of COPD and death years before any COPD diagnosis. (PMIDs 42578378, 42838239; first seen 2026-11-04)
- **tricuspid-regurgitation-late-systemic-disease** — Severe tricuspid regurgitation is usually documented when right heart failure is advanced, and systemic congestion markers predict death or heart failure hospitalization. (PMIDs 42901428, 42944652; first seen 2026-10-27)
- **platelet-thromboinflammation-copd** — Platelet activation markers predict COPD exacerbations and death in smokers, and cardiovascular risk concentrates in the weeks after a severe exacerbation. (PMIDs 42690694, 42696427, 42800650; first seen 2026-09-10)
- **early-phase-pah-therapeutics** — Emerging pulmonary vascular therapies, including activin-pathway agents and denervation, rest on small, open-label or retrospective single-center studies with surrogate endpoints and short follow-up. (PMIDs 42924171, 42728065, 42463617, 42908309, 42948081; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **body-shape-and-adherence-beyond-standard-metrics** — Adiposity measures relate to cardiovascular outcomes against expectation: central adiposity beats body mass index for mortality in reduced ejection fraction, and higher body mass index tracks with lower inpatient venous thromboembolism. (PMIDs 42340801, 42855850, 42862778, 42885756; first seen 2026-10-15)
