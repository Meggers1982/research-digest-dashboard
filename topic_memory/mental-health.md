# Topic Memory: Mental Health & Psychiatry
_Last updated: 2026-09-14 · source_id: mental-health · covers studies through 2026-09-13_

## Established findings
- **structural-barriers-persist-despite-policy** — Fragmented services, distance, racism and legal-system gaps continue to block mental health care for marginalized groups even where affirming laws, telehealth or protective legislation exist. (PMIDs 41553871, 42568194, 42674132, 42700306, 42517456, 42696107, 42263486, 42658622)
- **eeg-biomarkers-preliminary** — Candidate EEG, eye-tracking and PET markers for depression, ADHD, autism and dementia report promising accuracy from small single-center samples with weak effects and no external validation. (PMIDs 42671816, 42090937, 40350038, 42684535, 42698280)

## Emerging threads
- **personalized-digital-tools-no-objective-gain** — Structured psychological and digital interventions repeatedly fail to outperform simpler comparators, natural recovery or active controls, sometimes adding distress despite subjective improvement. (PMIDs 42142506, 40856349, 42492314, 42561812, 41588739, 42658633; first seen 2026-09-05)
- **drug-naive-first-episode-biology** — Medication-naive first-episode psychosis, mania and depression show peripheral immune, metabolic and neural abnormalities at presentation, indicating biology that precedes treatment rather than following it. (PMIDs 42112712, 42692293, 42579964, 42686075, 42673683; first seen 2026-09-01)
- **insulin-resistance-drug-naive-schizophrenia** — Large Chinese cohorts of antipsychotic-naive first-episode schizophrenia link high fasting insulin and insulin resistance to cognitive impairment and sex-specific prolactin-glucose patterns. (PMIDs 42691514, 42696132; first seen 2026-09-06)
- **schizophrenia-specific-cognitive-decline** — Cognitive and fine motor decline in long-term psychosis cohorts is progressive and largely specific to schizophrenia, tracking neurodevelopmental polygenic risk decades later. (PMIDs 39503784, 42050012, 42696130; first seen 2026-09-04)
- **sgm-disparities-despite-supportive-context** — Transgender and sexual minority adults show roughly double the mental health burden and measurable economic disadvantage even in protective legislative or affirming health-system contexts. (PMIDs 42658622, 42693312, 41553871; first seen 2026-09-03)
- **relational-context-perinatal-distress** — Perinatal anxiety and parental burnout track partner and family context — parenting alliance, partner ADHD, blame after pregnancy loss — rather than individual symptoms alone. (PMIDs 39773292, 41982066, 42693223; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **infant-markers-predict-later-behavior** — One-month resting brain activity and poorer measured sleep across the first year predict later socioemotional problems and increasing temperamental unpredictability. (PMIDs 42671816, 42155311; first seen 2026-09-06)
- **ai-governance-and-cultural-fit** — Reviews of AI therapy and AI-assisted clinical tools converge on unresolved cultural blind spots, consent and liability questions that deployment is outpacing. (PMIDs 42492314, 42398398; first seen 2026-09-05)
- **sudep-counseling-gap** — Many epilepsy clinicians lack formal SUDEP training and skip guideline counseling, while a pilot showed explicit SUDEP discussion with adolescents did not raise distress. (PMIDs 42684085, 42208388; first seen 2026-09-08)
