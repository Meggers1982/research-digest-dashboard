# Topic Memory: Mental Health & Psychiatry
_Last updated: 2026-10-05 · source_id: mental-health · covers studies through 2026-10-04_

## Established findings
- **structural-barriers-persist-despite-policy** — Fragmented services, paperwork, distance, incarceration, migration status and prescribing inertia continue to block or distort mental health and addiction care for marginalized groups even where affirming policy exists. (PMIDs 41553871, 42568194, 42674132, 42700306, 42815105, 42537444, 42413308, 42765972)
- **eeg-biomarkers-preliminary** — Candidate EEG, imaging and PET markers for depression, psychosis, ADHD and dementia report promising accuracy from small single-center samples with weak effects and no external validation. (PMIDs 42671816, 42090937, 40350038, 42684535, 42698280, 42763057, 42762746, 42772657)
- **personalized-digital-tools-no-objective-gain** — Psychological, digital and mindfulness interventions, and subjective self-reports of cognition, change awareness or perceived experience without matching gains in measured behavior or cognition. (PMIDs 42142506, 40856349, 42492314, 42561812, 41588739, 42777609, 42791633)
- **maternal-perinatal-depression-infant-outcomes** — Maternal perinatal depression and postnatal psychiatric hospitalization predict infant EEG asymmetry, difficult temperament, developmental delay and more preventable child hospitalizations, partly through bonding difficulties. (PMIDs 42771989, 42778033, 42779328, 42811250)

## Emerging threads
- **drug-naive-first-episode-biology** — Medication-naive first-episode psychosis and depression show immune, endocrine, metabolic, genetic and glymphatic abnormalities at presentation, indicating biology that precedes treatment rather than following it. (PMIDs 42112712, 42692293, 42579964, 42686075, 42673683, 42762626, 42800290, 42766101; first seen 2026-09-01)
- **infant-markers-predict-later-behavior** — Infant resting and auditory-evoked brain activity and poorer measured sleep in the first year predict later socioemotional problems and anxiety lasting into adolescence. (PMIDs 42671816, 41547388; first seen 2026-09-06)
- **first-responder-organizational-distress** — Distress in first responders and crisis workers is driven more by organizational culture, stigma and system failure than trauma exposure, and peer-support protocols go largely unused. (PMIDs 41940892, 42712060, 42811627, 42785918; first seen 2026-10-01)
- **autism-misrecognized-adult-psychiatry** — Adult autistic features are often unrecognized or misattributed to psychotic illness, show distinct polygenic roots from psychosis, and drive adults toward self-diagnosis. (PMIDs 42770208, 42771106, 42143913; first seen 2026-09-29)
- **psychological-stress-stroke-risk** — Large meta-analyses link clinically diagnosed anxiety, PTSD and chronic work-related stress to roughly 27-50% higher risk of first-ever stroke, independent of depression and cardiac disease. (PMIDs 42767516, 42778898; first seen 2026-09-26)
- **relational-context-youth-internalizing** — Adolescent anxiety, depression and bullying track the surrounding relational climate — teacher emotions, school cohesion and parent-teen discrepancies — rather than individual symptoms alone. (PMIDs 42765430, 42753049, 42782495, 40338513; first seen 2026-09-23)
- **relational-context-perinatal-distress** — Perinatal anxiety and parental burnout track partner and family context — parenting alliance, partner ADHD, blame after pregnancy loss — rather than individual symptoms alone. (PMIDs 39773292, 41982066, 42693223; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **insulin-resistance-drug-naive-schizophrenia** — Large Chinese cohorts of antipsychotic-naive first-episode schizophrenia link high fasting insulin and insulin resistance to cognitive impairment and sex-specific prolactin-glucose patterns. (PMIDs 42691514, 42696132; first seen 2026-09-06)
- **schizophrenia-specific-cognitive-decline** — Cognitive and fine motor decline in long-term psychosis cohorts is progressive and largely specific to schizophrenia, tracking neurodevelopmental polygenic risk decades later. (PMIDs 39503784, 42050012, 42696130; first seen 2026-09-04)
- **sgm-disparities-despite-supportive-context** — Transgender and sexual minority adults show roughly double the mental health burden and measurable economic disadvantage even in protective legislative or affirming health-system contexts. (PMIDs 42658622, 42693312, 41553871; first seen 2026-09-03)
