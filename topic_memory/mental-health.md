# Topic Memory: Mental Health & Psychiatry
_Last updated: 2026-09-28 · source_id: mental-health · covers studies through 2026-09-27_

## Established findings
- **structural-barriers-persist-despite-policy** — Fragmented services, distance, incarceration, racism and legal-system bias continue to block or distort mental health care for marginalized groups even where affirming laws and telehealth exist. (PMIDs 41553871, 42568194, 42674132, 42700306, 42517456, 42696107, 42263486, 42658622, 42771056, 42765972)
- **eeg-biomarkers-preliminary** — Candidate EEG, imaging and PET markers for depression, psychosis, ADHD and dementia report promising accuracy from small single-center samples with weak effects and no external validation. (PMIDs 42671816, 42090937, 40350038, 42684535, 42698280, 42763057, 42762746)
- **personalized-digital-tools-no-objective-gain** — Structured psychological, digital and symbolic interventions repeatedly fail to outperform active controls on objective outcomes, changing awareness or subjective experience without measurable behavioral or cognitive gain. (PMIDs 42142506, 40856349, 42492314, 42561812, 41588739, 42658633, 42777609, 42791336)

## Emerging threads
- **drug-naive-first-episode-biology** — Medication-naive first-episode psychosis, mania and depression show immune, metabolic, cerebrospinal-fluid and neural abnormalities at presentation, indicating biology that precedes treatment rather than following it. (PMIDs 42112712, 42692293, 42579964, 42686075, 42673683, 42763057, 42762626, 42764133, 42762746; first seen 2026-09-01)
- **maternal-perinatal-depression-infant-outcomes** — Maternal depressive symptoms in pregnancy and early infancy predict infant frontal EEG asymmetry, difficult temperament and developmental delay, with bonding difficulties mediating part of the association. (PMIDs 42771989, 42778033, 42779328; first seen 2026-09-23)
- **psychological-stress-stroke-risk** — Large meta-analyses link clinically diagnosed anxiety, PTSD and chronic work-related stress to roughly 27-50% higher risk of first-ever stroke, independent of depression and cardiac disease. (PMIDs 42767516, 42778898; first seen 2026-09-26)
- **relational-context-youth-internalizing** — Adolescent anxiety and depression track the surrounding relational climate — teacher emotions and parent-teen discrepancies in perceived parenting — rather than individual symptoms alone. (PMIDs 42765430, 42753049; first seen 2026-09-23)
- **relational-context-perinatal-distress** — Perinatal anxiety and parental burnout track partner and family context — parenting alliance, partner ADHD, blame after pregnancy loss — rather than individual symptoms alone. (PMIDs 39773292, 41982066, 42693223; first seen 2026-09-08)
- **insulin-resistance-drug-naive-schizophrenia** — Large Chinese cohorts of antipsychotic-naive first-episode schizophrenia link high fasting insulin and insulin resistance to cognitive impairment and sex-specific prolactin-glucose patterns. (PMIDs 42691514, 42696132; first seen 2026-09-06)
- **schizophrenia-specific-cognitive-decline** — Cognitive and fine motor decline in long-term psychosis cohorts is progressive and largely specific to schizophrenia, tracking neurodevelopmental polygenic risk decades later. (PMIDs 39503784, 42050012, 42696130; first seen 2026-09-04)
- **sgm-disparities-despite-supportive-context** — Transgender and sexual minority adults show roughly double the mental health burden and measurable economic disadvantage even in protective legislative or affirming health-system contexts. (PMIDs 42658622, 42693312, 41553871; first seen 2026-09-03)
- **infant-markers-predict-later-behavior** — One-month resting brain activity and poorer measured sleep across the first year predict later socioemotional problems and increasing temperamental unpredictability. (PMIDs 42671816, 42155311; first seen 2026-09-06)
- **ai-governance-and-cultural-fit** — Reviews of AI therapy and AI-assisted clinical tools converge on unresolved cultural blind spots, consent and liability questions that deployment is outpacing. (PMIDs 42492314, 42398398; first seen 2026-09-05)
