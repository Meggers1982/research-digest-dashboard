# Topic Memory: Mental Health & Psychiatry
_Last updated: 2026-10-23 · source_id: mental-health · covers studies through 2026-10-18_

## Established findings
- **structural-barriers-persist-despite-policy** — Fragmented services, paperwork, distance, incarceration, migration status and prescribing inertia continue to block or distort mental health and addiction care for marginalized groups even where affirming policy exists. (PMIDs 41553871, 42568194, 42674132, 42700306, 42815105, 42537444, 42413308, 42765972, 42837993)
- **eeg-biomarkers-preliminary** — Candidate EEG, imaging and PET markers for depression, psychosis, ADHD and dementia report promising accuracy from small single-center samples with weak effects and no external validation. (PMIDs 42671816, 42090937, 40350038, 42684535, 42698280, 42763057, 42762746, 42772657, 42863800)
- **personalized-digital-tools-no-objective-gain** — Psychological, digital and mindfulness interventions change subjective self-reports of cognition, change awareness or perceived experience without matching gains in measured behavior or cognition. (PMIDs 42142506, 40856349, 42492314, 42561812, 41588739, 42777609, 42791633)
- **maternal-perinatal-depression-infant-outcomes** — Maternal perinatal depression and postnatal psychiatric hospitalization predict infant EEG asymmetry, difficult temperament, developmental delay and more preventable child hospitalizations, partly through bonding difficulties. (PMIDs 42771989, 42778033, 42779328, 42811250)

## Emerging threads
- **drug-naive-first-episode-biology** — Medication-naive first-episode psychosis and depression show immune, endocrine, metabolic, genetic, molecular and sleep abnormalities at presentation, indicating biology that precedes treatment and tracks symptom severity and early response. (PMIDs 42112712, 42692293, 42579964, 42686075, 42673683, 42762626, 42800290, 42766101, 42691514, 42696132, 42859188, 42874278; first seen 2026-09-01)
- **first-responder-organizational-distress** — Distress in first responders and crisis workers is driven more by organizational culture, stigma and system failure than trauma exposure, and peer-support protocols go largely unused. (PMIDs 41940892, 42712060, 42811627, 42785918; first seen 2026-10-01)
- **autism-misrecognized-adult-psychiatry** — Adult autistic features are often unrecognized or misattributed to psychotic illness, show distinct polygenic roots from psychosis, and drive adults toward self-diagnosis. (PMIDs 42770208, 42771106, 42143913; first seen 2026-09-29)
- **psychological-stress-stroke-risk** — Large meta-analyses link clinically diagnosed anxiety, PTSD and chronic work-related stress to roughly 27-50% higher risk of first-ever stroke, independent of depression and cardiac disease. (PMIDs 42767516, 42778898; first seen 2026-09-26)
- **relational-context-youth-internalizing** — Adolescent anxiety, depression and bullying track the surrounding relational climate — teacher emotions, school cohesion and parent-teen discrepancies — rather than individual symptoms alone. (PMIDs 42765430, 42753049, 42782495, 40338513; first seen 2026-09-23)
- **sgm-disparities-despite-supportive-context** — Transgender and sexual minority adults show roughly double the mental health burden and measurable economic disadvantage even in protective or affirming contexts, and standard symptom tools miss their presentations. (PMIDs 42658622, 42693312, 41553871, 42841447; first seen 2026-09-03)
- **adult-seizure-diagnosis-delay** — Adults with seizure and seizure-like disorders face prolonged misdiagnosis from pediatric-derived criteria, limited clinician knowledge and unrecognized warning presentations such as falls. (PMIDs 42836841, 42837993, 42848964; first seen 2026-10-13)
- **caregiver-burden-unsupported** — Family care partners in severe psychiatric and neurological illness report lasting distress with little guidance, and the unpaid caregiving role falls about twice as often on women. (PMIDs 42864277, 42837993; first seen 2026-10-13)
- **emotion-avoidance-behavioral-addiction** — Difficulty identifying emotions and experiential avoidance, rather than anxiety symptoms themselves, account for much of the link between distress and problematic internet, gambling and substance use. (PMIDs 42860713, 42611548; first seen 2026-10-12)
- **fep-outcomes-social-determinants** — After first-episode psychosis, socioeconomic status and older age at onset predict residual symptoms and poorer functional recovery better than clinical baseline variables. (PMIDs 42854300, 42853269; first seen 2026-10-15)
